```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Majd is a backend developer in a team of four at a translation agency. He tells a founder: 'We moved the file hand-off to a shared folder with a script, and it made hand-offs about three times faster.' The founder asks: 'Three times faster than what? And what was your part?' Majd's facts: he wrote the script himself, and the team chose the shared folder. The agency's order log shows about 30 minutes per hand-off in the three weeks before, and about 10 minutes in the three weeks after. Which answer uses these facts?"
options:
  - "We did it as a team, and I think hand-offs went from 30 minutes to 10, so about two thirds less."
  - "I wrote the script and the team chose the folder. The order log shows about 30 minutes before and 10 after, three weeks each."
  - "I wrote the script and the team chose the folder. Hand-offs are now about three times faster, which was a big gain for the agency."
  - "I wrote the script and the team chose the folder. Hand-offs went from 30 minutes to 10, and everyone in the agency was happy."
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "'We' hides his own part, and 'I think' shows he has no source for the numbers. The founder's question 'what was your part?' is not answered."
  - "Right. It separates his part from the team's, and it gives a before, an after, a time window and a source: the order log."
  - "His part and the team's part are clear, but 'three times faster' still has no before. The founder's 'than what?' stays unanswered."
  - "His part, the before and the after are there, but there is no time window and no source. The founder will ask 'how do you know?' next."
```

```quiz

question: "Aya is a frontend developer at a small web studio. A founder asks why she moved the delivery fee to the first screen of an online shop's checkout. She chose to show the fee as a line under the cart total, not as a pop-up. She has no test results about what shoppers prefer. Which answer is the real reason, and still holds at the next why?"
options:
  - "My lead asked me after a client complained about a fee. Inside that, I chose a line under the total, not a pop-up."
  - "Everyone knows shoppers prefer to see fees early, so it was simply the obvious choice for any checkout page like this one."
  - "It was my lead's decision, so I cannot say anything at all about why the checkout page looks the way it does now."
  - "I decided myself that surprise fees are a main reason shoppers leave a shop, so I moved the fee to the first screen."
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. It gives the reason she had at the time, says what was her lead's call, and names the part she decided herself. Every next why can be answered from it."
  - "This is a tidy reason that sounds sure, but she has no test results. The next 'how do you know?' breaks it."
  - "Honest about the lead's call, but it gives up the part she did decide: the line under the total instead of a pop-up."
  - "This reason appears only now and contradicts the facts. Her lead asked her, so saying she decided alone is a reason invented afterwards."
```

```quiz
  - "A rebuild would use the developer's two weeks on one guess, and it does not check which change cost the orders."
  - "This avoids a decision. The founder asked what Mustafa would do, and waiting names no option, no number and no line for changing course."
  - "Right. It states an assumption, picks the cheaper check, names the number to watch and says what he would do if it does not move."
```

```quiz
  - "It lists tools and work done, but it never says what problem it solved for the shop staff or what changed for them."
  - "Right. It gives the problem in the shop's terms, what he built in one sentence, the key choice and the result, all in words a founder can follow."
```
