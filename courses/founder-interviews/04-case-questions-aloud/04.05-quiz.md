```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Iyad is asked: 'Our delivery drivers are late more often. You start next week.' He asks which days and which areas. The founder says: 'Mostly Fridays, in the east of the city.' What should Iyad do next?"
options:
  - "Ask about the company's history, funding and plans for the future"
  - "Say the problem looks like Fridays in the east, and look there first"
  - "Go back to his first plan and check every day in every area equally"
  - "Say he cannot start until the founder shares all of the delivery data"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "These questions would not change what Iyad does first. The founder has just given him a place to look."
  - "Right. It shows that he heard the answer, and it turns the answer into a first step. A clarifying question is worth asking only if the answer changes the plan."
  - "This ignores the answer. The founder said where the problem is, and checking everything equally throws that away."
  - "A case is meant to be worked with missing facts. He has enough to name a first step, and he can say what he would ask for next."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Lama practises with an AI chat tool. The case: 'Our app's sign-ups fell by a third last month. What would you do?' The AI suggests this answer: 'I would improve onboarding, run tests, tighten the funnel, add game features and personalise emails, and I would measure engagement. Many tactics show breadth.' What is the mistake?"
options:
  - "A list gives no choice, no number to watch and no line to change course"
  - "It should list even more tactics, so the founder sees every idea she has"
  - "It uses too few technical words for a product role in a startup team"
  - "It is right, because many tactics show more thinking than one choice"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. A founder with one developer needs to hear what Lama would do first, how she would know it worked, and what result would make her stop. The list gives none of these."
  - "More tactics would make the problem worse. The answer already hands the choosing back to the founder."
  - "The answer has plenty of technical words already, and they do not help. A founder can follow a plain answer with a choice in it."
  - "Many tactics can look like thinking, but a small team can do only one or two things at a time. The founder asked what she would do."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Mazen tests a free-shipping banner. Before the test, 12 percent of visitors bought. He set his line at 15 percent. The normal swing is about 1 point. After four weeks, 17 percent bought. What should he conclude?"
options:
  - "Change course, because the rate did not reach 20 percent after four weeks"
  - "Too close to call, because any rise in a month could be down to chance"
  - "Keep it: 17 is 2 points over the line of 15, more than one swing"
  - "Keep it, because 17 is higher than the starting rate of 12 percent"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "His line was 15, not 20. A line has to be set before the result, and it cannot grow afterwards."
  - "The result is 2 points above the line, and the normal swing is 1 point. That is too far above to put down to chance."
  - "Right. The result is above the line he set in advance by more than one normal swing, so he keeps the change."
  - "The rate did rise from 12, but his test was whether it beat the line of 15 by more than chance. A rise alone does not answer that."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Anas is told: 'You have one developer for one week.' He wants to give three options for a drop in sign-ups. Which of these is an option he could start on Monday?"
options:
  - "Improve the onboarding experience for new users of the app overall"
  - "Increase customer engagement over the next few months of the year"
  - "Make the whole app feel more modern and friendly for every user"
  - "Add a progress bar to the 5-step sign-up, about two days of work"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "This is an idea. Nobody could say what to do on Monday, or when it is finished."
  - "This is a goal, not an action. It has no first step and no end."
  - "This is an idea about a feeling. It cannot be started, finished or checked in a week."
  - "Right. It is one action, it fits the week, and it has a size. A founder can say yes or no to it."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Ruba is asked: 'Support emails about late parcels doubled this month. You start next week.' She finds that parcels leave late on Mondays and Thursdays, when one of the shop's two packers is off. Late parcels are about 9 a week now. Which answer to 'What would you do first?' is best?"
options:
  - "Hire a second courier, because couriers are the most common cause of late parcels"
  - "Add a packer on Mondays and Thursdays; 9 late a week now, keep it if under 5"
  - "It depends on many things, so I would study the problem for a month first"
  - "Tell customers that parcels may be late, then see if the emails go down"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "Nothing in her facts points to the couriers. The late days match the days a packer is off."
  - "Right. It starts from the days the facts point to, names the number with its value now, and gives a line. The founder can say yes or no and can check it later."
  - "A month of study is not a first step. The facts she has already point to two days of the week."
  - "This treats the emails and leaves the late parcels. There is no number to watch and no line to change course."
```
