```quiz
type: mcq
id: skill-slot-time-q1
question: A provider works every Monday at 09:00 in Asia/Gaza. Which fields should SkillSlot keep for the repeating availability rule?
options:
  - A fixed UTC timestamp for next Monday only
  - Weekday, local 09:00, and Asia/Gaza
  - Local 09:00 and today's numeric UTC offset
  - The client's browser-local version of 09:00
correct: 1
explanations:
  - One dated UTC timestamp cannot express a repeating local rule.
  - The weekday, local time and IANA zone let each dated occurrence be resolved with its own rules.
  - Today's offset may differ on a future date and cannot replace the zone.
  - The client zone is only a display choice and cannot own provider availability.
```

```quiz
type: mcq
id: skill-slot-time-q2
question: A client changes their display zone after paying for a lesson. What must happen to Booking.startAt?
options:
  - It moves by the difference between the old and new UTC offsets
  - It stays the same UTC instant; the label is formatted again
  - It is replaced by the provider's weekly rule
  - It is saved as an unzoned local string for the reminder worker
correct: 1
explanations:
  - An offset difference changes presentation, not the accepted appointment.
  - A paid booking has one fixed instant unless the parties explicitly reschedule.
  - A weekly rule generates offers; it does not replace an existing booking.
  - An unzoned string cannot tell a worker when to fire reliably.
```

```quiz
type: mcq
id: skill-slot-time-q3
question: In America/New_York on 3 November 2024, the local time 01:30 occurs twice. What does SkillSlot's first-version policy do with a provider boundary at 01:30?
options:
  - Choose the earlier occurrence and hide its offset from the provider
  - Choose the later occurrence without recording that choice
  - Reject the boundary and request an explicit offset choice
  - Save the 01:30 wall-clock label as a UTC start instant
correct: 2
explanations:
  - Silent selection can book a different hour from the provider's intention.
  - Choosing the other occurrence silently has the same defect.
  - The boundary maps to two instants, so the product needs an explicit policy and clear feedback.
  - The local clock label is not a UTC timestamp.
```

```quiz
type: short
id: skill-slot-time-q4
question: Why does a reminder worker schedule from Booking.startAt instead of recalculating the provider's weekly rule?
allowSpeechInput: true
modelAnswer: Booking.startAt is the fixed UTC instant the parties accepted. A weekly rule can generate future offers with changed offsets or edits; recalculating it could move a paid appointment or reminder.
```
