```quiz
type: mcq
id: sufra-jobs-q1
question: Lina's two-kitchen checkout has not claimed Kitchen B yet. Which work may be queued before showing a payment form?
options:
  - Claim B later and offer the form now
  - Notify B now and check capacity later
  - Finish both claims before offering payment
  - Generate all future windows after charging
correct: 2
explanations:
  - Payment cannot begin while a kitchen's capacity is unknown.
  - A cook must not receive an unpaid or partial job.
  - The prepared attempt requires all claims and drafts before payment.
  - Future generation is unrelated to this checkout decision.
```

```quiz
type: mcq
id: sufra-jobs-q2
question: An unpaid claim expires while the worker is stopped. What should recovery inspect after restart?
options:
  - The browser's last visible countdown only
  - The claim's stored deadline and held state
  - Redis's last job log without the window row
  - The cook's weekly template without the claim
correct: 1
explanations:
  - A browser timer is not an authority for capacity release.
  - The dated claim records both when and whether release is still allowed.
  - A job log does not prove the claim is still held.
  - The weekly template does not own mutable dated capacity.
```

```quiz
type: mcq
id: sufra-jobs-q3
question: The nightly generator runs twice on a window with a held claim. Which write pattern preserves the promise?
options:
  - Replace the full window from the weekly rule
  - Reset remaining units to template capacity
  - Insert another row with a random start time
  - Upsert insert-only fields under a unique key
correct: 3
explanations:
  - Replacement could erase the held claim and its counter debit.
  - Resetting units makes already held effort available again.
  - A random time creates a second public promise.
  - The unique kitchen/start pair and insert-only fields leave existing claims unchanged.
```
