```quiz
type: mcq
id: sufra-payment-q1
question: A two-kitchen attempt has a held claim for Kitchen A, but Kitchen B's claim failed. One draft order exists and the browser asks for payment. What should the API do?
options:
  - Create one intent for Kitchen A's saved share
  - Wait for Stripe, then try Kitchen B again
  - Refuse payment and compensate the held claim
  - Create two intents and cancel one if needed
correct: 2
explanations:
  - The diner reviewed one all-kitchen cart, not a smaller purchase.
  - Stripe cannot decide whether Kitchen B has cooking units.
  - A partial preparation never crosses the payable boundary, and the earlier hold is released.
  - Separate charges can leave the diner paying for a partial cart.
```

```quiz
type: mcq
id: sufra-payment-q2
question: Stripe sends a signed success event for the stored intent. The amount matches, but Kitchen B's claim is already released after expiry. What is the safe outcome?
options:
  - Restore the released claim and place both orders
  - Place Kitchen A's order and refund Kitchen B
  - Ignore the charge because the hold has expired
  - Hide both jobs and start a full refund record
correct: 3
explanations:
  - A released claim cannot be silently revived after its units returned to the pool.
  - Lina paid for a one-interval combined cart, not an unreviewed partial order.
  - The successful charge is real and requires reconciliation.
  - The purchase is unfulfillable under this policy, so neither kitchen gets a job and the charge is refunded.
```

explanations:
