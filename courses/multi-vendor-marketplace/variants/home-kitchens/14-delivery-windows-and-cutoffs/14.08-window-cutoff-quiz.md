id: sufra-window-q1
question: A 13:00 lunch window has a 12:00 general cutoff and a 20-minute travel buffer. Maqluba needs 90 minutes of prep. When does maqluba close?
options:
  - At 11:10, before the kitchen's general cutoff
  - At 12:00, exactly when the kitchen closes orders
  - At 12:10, after preparation and travel are counted
  - At 13:00, when the delivery window begins
correct: 0
explanations:
  - Subtracting 110 minutes from 13:00 gives 11:10, earlier than the general cutoff.
  - The general cutoff is too late for this slow dish.
  - Adding the durations after noon reverses the deadline calculation.
  - A dish cannot first be ordered when delivery is supposed to begin.

```quiz
type: mcq
id: sufra-window-q2
question: The seven-day generator runs again after a diner holds two capacity units. Which update is safe for the existing dated window?
options:
  - Reset remaining units to the kitchen's weekly capacity
  - Replace its UTC start with the newest guessed offset
  - Keep the row and its held units unchanged on retry
  - Delete it and insert a new row with a fresh ID
correct: 2
explanations:
  - Resetting units can make held capacity available twice.
  - A dated promise cannot be silently shifted by a guessed offset.
  - An idempotent retry preserves the existing row, claims, and remaining units.
  - Deleting the row can erase links from claims and paid orders.
```

```quiz
type: mcq
id: sufra-window-q3
question: A cook closes tomorrow's lunch after one diner has paid. What should Sufra do with that dated window?
options:
  - Delete the window and hide the paid order from the cook
  - Close it to new orders, keep the paid row, and start resolution
  - Move the paid order to a new window without asking the diner
  - Reset the row's remaining units and rerun generation
correct: 1
explanations:
  - Deleting the row loses the promise and the evidence needed to resolve it.
  - New claims stop while the original paid record remains for contact or cancellation.
  - A diner paid for a particular interval; moving it needs an explicit flow.
  - Resetting capacity could oversell and does not resolve the paid order.
```
