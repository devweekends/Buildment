```quiz
type: mcq
question: "A design has one CDN, two app servers, a database and a queue with two workers. It passes every check in the peak scene and fails the check in the scene where a CDN network goes down. The board shows 0.0% served. What explains it?"
options:
  - "The CDN is too small and drops requests when it is busy"
  - "The client has no other way in when its one CDN stops"
  - "The load balancer becomes the busiest part in that scene"
  - "The scene also removes the app servers behind the CDN"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "A CDN handles a million requests a second on the board, far above 1,500. Its size is not the problem. The failure appears only when it is gone."
  - "Right. Every request enters through the CDN. With the one CDN gone there is no path left, so nothing is served. A second CDN, linked the same way, takes the whole load."
  - "The load balancer is behind the CDN. With the CDN down it receives nothing, so it cannot be the busiest part."
  - "The scene only takes the CDN down. The app servers are fine, but no request reaches them."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Conversion jobs arrive at 750 a second. The workers stop for 60 seconds, then return. Each worker handles 2,400 jobs a second. How many workers clear the waiting jobs (the backlog) within 20 seconds?"
options:
  - "One, because it handles more than 750 a second"
  - "Two, with 4,050 a second to spare"
  - "Three, because the board needs one per 1,000 jobs"
  - "None, because a queue clears itself on restart"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "One worker has 1,650 a second to spare after the new jobs. The backlog of 45,000 takes 28 seconds, over the 20 allowed."
  - "Right. Two workers handle 4,800 a second. After 750 for new jobs, 4,050 a second are spare, and 45,000 divided by 4,050 is about 12 seconds."
  - "There is no such rule. Three workers also pass, but they cost more than the lab allows, and the cost check fails."
  - "A queue only holds jobs. Something must take them out, and it must do so faster than they arrive, or the backlog stays."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "A learner sets both CDN hit rates to 0.95, presses Run, and every check passes for $5.47 an hour. Lamha's logs say the real hit rate is 0.5. What should the learner conclude?"
options:
  - "The design is cheaper than the reference and should be used"
  - "The CDN hit rate is too low in the logs and should be raised"
  - "Two CDNs are not needed when the hit rate is high"
  - "The board cannot check a hit rate, so the pass is empty"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "It is cheaper only because it assumes a hit rate the service does not have. At the real 0.5, the same parts would face ten times the load they were sized for."
  - "A hit rate is measured, not chosen. The logs say 0.5 because that is how often the CDN already has the video. Wishing it higher does not change what viewers watch."
  - "The second CDN is there for a different reason: one network can fail. A high hit rate does not stop an outage."
  - "Right. The board takes the number you type. A pass built on a hit rate you cannot reach is a pass on a different service, so set the number from the logs."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "On the board, the two CDNs and object storage are more than three quarters of the hourly cost, and a CDN costs more per request than storage. Why is that a misleading picture of a real video platform?"
options:
  - "Real CDNs are free, so only storage should cost money"
  - "The board has no bytes, and bytes are the real cost"
  - "Real storage costs more than a CDN for every request"
  - "The board hides the servers, which are the real cost"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "CDNs are not free. They charge for requests and, much more, for the data they send."
  - "Right. The board prices each request and sends no data. In real life the bytes dominate the bill, and a CDN pays for itself by answering them near the viewer. The board cannot see that."
  - "That is not the point. In real life a CDN is cheaper than going straight to storage for the same bytes, and the board has no bytes to compare."
  - "The servers are on the board, and they are a small part of the bill. The missing piece is the data sent, not the servers."
```
