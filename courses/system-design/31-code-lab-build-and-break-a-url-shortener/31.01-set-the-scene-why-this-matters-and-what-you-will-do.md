In the visual lab you built a short-link service from boxes, and a board told you whether it passed. A board only claims that a design works. It cannot tell you whether it does. In this module you stop guessing: you run the same design as real programs on your laptop, change one thing at a time, and read what happens.

Before you touch anything, make a guess. Say a friend sends you a short link. On a normal day, 98 out of 100 times the answer is found in the cache (fast memory), and only 2 out of 100 times the database has to be asked. Now someone switches the cache off.

It is the same design as the visual lab: a gateway, three copies of the API, Redis, a worker and Postgres, all started with one Docker command. You can click on every part, read the code of each one, and stop any of them on purpose.

<GoDeeper title="Go deeper: how this module is built">

The module has the same shape as the visual lab before it. This chapter asks you to guess. The next one gets the stack running. Then you get a list of tasks with directions and no answers: change one thing, say what you expect, run it, and read the number. After that you compare your numbers with the ones measured on the real stack and see which check on the board each task mirrors. The worked solutions are kept apart, in the repository, and you open them on purpose. The last chapter asks you to explain the result in your own words and to say how it compared with your guess.

The repository is public: github.com/ZeeshanAdilButt/system-design-labs, folder `labs/lab-1-url-shortener`. Everything you need is in it, including a contract file that lists the same checks the visual lab uses, so a design you built on the board can be replayed here.

</GoDeeper>

## Why a board is not enough

<MermaidDiagram title="Board and real stack" chart={`flowchart LR
  V["Visual lab<br>a model: fast, safe, a guess"] --> C["Code lab<br>real programs: slow, honest, measured"]`} />

The board is a model. It is fast and safe, and its numbers are made up to compare designs. This lab is the honest half. Its numbers were measured on a real laptop, so they depend on your machine, but their shape is real.

<GoDeeper title="Go deeper: what the board could not show">

The board cannot show how a real cache is filled and emptied, how long a database takes to refuse a request when it is down, or what happens to clicks when the cache and the click queue share one Redis. In the visual lab you were asked what you would change about that last one. Here you will stop Redis and count the clicks you lost.

</GoDeeper>
