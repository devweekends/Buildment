```quiz
type: mcq
question: "A design has no queue and no workers. The board shows $2.39 an hour and a slowest 1 in 100 of 87 ms, against $2.93 and 125 ms for the reference design. It fails two checks. What explains the lower cost and the lower p99?"
options:
  - "The search nodes are faster when they are not shared"
  - "The index takes no product changes, so it does less"
  - "The queue adds a delay to every search, which it removes"
  - "The workers use the cache, which is now free for searches"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "Search nodes are the same speed. They are less busy only because they have less to do, and that is the point."
  - "Right. Without a queue and workers, no product change reaches the index, so the nodes answer 2,000 lookups a second instead of 4,000. The design is cheaper and faster because it skips a job, and the change check fails."
  - "The queue is off the search path. A search never waits for it, so removing it cannot speed searches up."
  - "Workers write to the index. They do not use the cache, and the cache has no part in this."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Product changes arrive at 2,000 a second. The indexing worker stops for 30 seconds and then returns. Each worker handles 2,400 changes a second. How many workers clear the waiting changes (the backlog) within 30 seconds of returning?"
options:
  - "One, because it is faster than the arrival rate"
  - "Two, with 2,800 a second to spare"
  - "Three, because the board needs one for every 1,000"
  - "None, because a queue clears itself on restart"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "One worker handles 2,400 a second against 2,000 arriving, so only 400 a second are spare. The 60,000 waiting changes take 150 seconds."
  - "Right. Two workers handle 4,800 a second. After 2,000 for new changes, 2,800 a second are spare, and 60,000 divided by 2,800 is about 22 seconds."
  - "There is no such rule. Three workers also pass and clear the backlog in 12 seconds, but they cost $0.12 an hour more than the lab needs."
  - "A queue only holds changes. Something must take them out, and faster than they arrive, or the waiting changes stay."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Ten memory-small search nodes and five memory-medium search nodes cost the same and both pass at peak. One node dies in each design. Which design is slower afterwards, and why?"
options:
  - "The five nodes, because a loss takes a fifth of the fleet"
  - "The ten nodes, because more nodes means more to coordinate"
  - "Neither, because the load is the same and the cost is equal"
  - "The ten nodes, because small machines are slower than medium"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. Losing one of five leaves four nodes at 83% busy and p99 near 200 ms. Losing one of ten leaves nine at 74% and p99 near 146 ms. Waiting grows faster than busyness, so the bigger share lost costs more."
  - "Coordination is real in a real cluster, but the board does not charge for it. In the model, ten nodes lose only a tenth and come out ahead."
  - "The load is the same, and it is shared by fewer machines afterwards. How many remain decides how busy they are."
  - "Size does not change how fast one lookup is in the model. A small node and a medium node answer at the same speed; the medium has twice the capacity."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "A team gets all twelve checks green. Two days after launch, shoppers complain that the best-selling red shoes are on page three of the results. What should the team conclude?"
options:
  - "The board is wrong, and the model must be fixed"
  - "The search index needs more nodes to rank well"
  - "The board checks load and survival, not result quality"
  - "The cache is returning stale suggestions to shoppers"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "The board did what it says. It has no ranking in it, so a good score says nothing about the order of results."
  - "More nodes would carry more load. They would not change which product is first, which is decided by how the products are scored."
  - "Right. The checks measure whether the index answers, in time, and survives a failure. Ranking quality needs its own tests, such as a set of queries with known good answers."
  - "Suggestions are the words offered while typing. They do not decide the order of search results."
```
