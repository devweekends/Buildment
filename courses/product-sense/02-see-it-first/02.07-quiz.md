```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Fares builds a booking page for a clinic desk. After a squint he lists four facts. 1) The clinic's name is the largest text on the page. 2) 'Book a visit' is a thin grey outline button near the bottom. 3) The opening hours are bold and look as strong as the button. 4) The page already uses three text sizes. The clinic wants visitors to book. Which change do the facts support?"
options:
  - "Add a fourth size, so that the hours stand apart"
  - "Make the name larger still, so it clearly comes first"
  - "Fill the button and set the hours in regular weight"
  - "Colour the hours bright red so people read them first"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "Fact 4 says the page already has three sizes, and more sizes would add noise. The hours also are not the goal."
  - "Fact 1 says the name is already the largest text. The squint problem is that the action the clinic wants is weak."
  - "Right. A filled button gives the booking action the strongest signal. Setting the hours in regular weight stops them from competing with it, and no new size is needed."
  - "The clinic wants bookings, not people reading the hours first. Bright red would make the hours the first thing and give red no clear job."
```

Which change do her checks call for?"

  - "Set a maximum width for the text so lines hold 45 to 90 characters"

  - "Block text scaling, so that the layout cannot change at 200 percent"

  - "Fact 2 says the line height is 1.5, a common default. Lowering it would not touch the long lines."
  - "Fact 3 says her page already passes at 200 percent. Blocking scaling would break a WCAG AA rule and help nobody."

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Abeer designs a booking list for a library room. She squints and sees one smear. Facts: 1) The lines inside each booking are 8 apart. 2) The gap between bookings is also 8. 3) Her spacing step is 8. Which pair of gaps fits?"
options:
  - "8 inside and 20 between, because 20 is clearly larger"
  - "16 inside and 8 between, because more air helps each line"
  - "8 inside and 8 between, because that is her step"
  - "8 inside and 24 between, both multiples of her step"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Twenty is larger, but it is not a multiple of her step of 8 (fact 3). A stray number breaks the rhythm that a step gives."
  - "This puts more space inside a booking than between bookings, which pulls each booking apart and joins the neighbours. The smear gets worse."
  - "These are the gaps she already has (facts 1 and 2). Equal gaps are the cause of the smear, and a step does not change that."
  - "Right. Eight inside and 24 between are both multiples of 8, and the space between is larger than the space inside, so the bookings show as groups."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Hiba's sports club page has body text at 15 px, regular, in #777777 on white. She asks an AI assistant to check it. It answers: 'This passes. #777777 on white is above the 4.5 to 1 bar, so no change is needed.' What is the mistake in this answer?"
options:
  - "There is no mistake, since grey that looks dark enough passes"
  - "The ratio is 4.48 to 1, below 4.5, so the text fails"
  - "It used the wrong bar, because all text needs 7 to 1 to pass"
  - "It missed that 15 px text is large, so it needs only 3 to 1"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "Looking dark enough is not the test. The formula gives a number, and #777777 on white comes out just below the bar."
  - "Right. The assistant did not compute the ratio. The formula gives 4.48 to 1 for #777777 on white, which is under 4.5, so the text fails and needs a darker grey such as #767676 or #595959."
  - "The AA bar for normal text is 4.5 to 1, not 7 to 1. The assistant named the right bar; its mistake was that it did not calculate the ratio."
  - "Large text is at least 18 point, or 14 point bold, which is about 24 or 18.5 px. Regular 15 px text is normal text."
```
