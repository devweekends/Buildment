```quiz
type: mcq
question: "A florist's page was made with an AI tool. Four facts: 1) The shop's sign and buckets are bright yellow, and the Order button uses the same yellow. 2) The page uses the phone's own font. 3) A banner reads 'Blooming with joy, every single day'. 4) Each bouquet shows a photo, a price and 'Order by 3 pm for same-day delivery'. Which change do the facts support?"
options:
  - "Replace the yellow Order button with a blue one"
  - "Replace the phone's own font with a font that is downloaded"
  - "Replace the banner with a fact about the flowers or delivery"
  - "Remove the price and the delivery line from each bouquet card"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "Fact 1 shows the yellow matches the sign and the buckets, so the florist can say why it is there. That makes it a choice."
  - "The phone's own font needs no download, and nothing in the facts says it fails a customer. A default is a fault only when it fails the person."
  - "Right. 'Blooming with joy, every single day' could sit on any florist's page. A fact such as a price, a delivery time or a flower in season gives the line a job."
  - "The price and 'Order by 3 pm for same-day delivery' are facts that only this shop can state. They should stay and move up, not go."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "An AI tool critiques a car wash page: 'Your page uses a purple gradient and the font Inter, which are signs of AI design. Remove both.' What is the right next check?"
options:
  - "Ask the car wash owner whether each one was chosen, and why"
  - "Remove both, and run the critique again to see what remains"
  - "Keep both, and ignore critiques from AI tools in future"
  - "Ask a second AI tool, and remove both if it agrees"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. Anthropic's own list calls these patterns defaults rather than choices. A gradient or a font with a reason tied to the car wash is a choice and can stay."
  - "The lists are not rules of design. A purple that matches the car wash's sign, with a reason, is a choice, and removing it without asking loses something."
  - "AI critique can be wrong, but not every point is. Each point is worth checking against a rule or a person before you keep or drop it."
  - "A second tool may repeat the same list, and agreement is not a reason. The check is the maker's own reason, or a rule that can be measured."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "A tool writes the top line for a laundry page: 'Experience seamless, effortless laundry that elevates your day.' What is the mistake in it?"
options:
  - "It uses words that only an AI tool would ever write"
  - "It is too short to describe the laundry in enough detail"
  - "It needs a plainer word than 'elevates', such as 'improves'"
  - "It could describe any laundry and holds no fact"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Word lists cannot label one text, and people use these words too. The problem is what the sentence tells the reader, not who wrote it."
  - "A longer line of the same kind is still empty. The sentence needs a price, a time or a place, not more words."
  - "Swapping one word keeps the same empty meaning. The sentence needs a fact a customer can check."
  - "Right. Swap 'laundry' for 'bakery' and the line still works. A price, a pickup time or a place would make it specific."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "A student shuttle page has one job: show the next departure and sell a ticket. Four versions were squinted at. Version 1: every line looked the same. Version 2: the next departure time stood out first, then the Buy button. Version 3: the logo and a photo of the bus stood out equally. Version 4: only the Buy button stood out. Which version should the team keep?"
options:
  - "Version 1, because every line is given equal weight"
  - "Version 2, because the time comes first and the button second"
  - "Version 3, because the photo makes the page feel friendly"
  - "Version 4, because the Buy button is the only action on the page"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "Version 1 had no first thing, so the eye has no place to start. Equal weight is the all-bold failure from chapter 5.4."
  - "Right. The departure time answers the job, and the button is the next step. One thing comes first, and it matches the job."
  - "A friendly feeling is not the job. In version 3 the first things are a logo and a photo, and the time is not among them."
  - "The Buy button matters, but a rider cannot choose a ticket without the time. Version 4 hides the fact the job depends on."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "A quiz app slides each question in from the right and bounces the score. A learner's phone is set to reduce motion. The maker still wants the app to feel alive. What is the best change?"
options:
  - "Keep the slides and the bounce, and make both shorter so that few readers notice them"
  - "Keep the bounce on the score because it is small, and remove only the slides"
  - "Drop the decorative motion, and say the result of a tap in words when the setting is on"
  - "Turn off all motion for everyone, even the change that answers a tap, to be safe"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "Shorter does not mean less. The reader asked for less motion, and motion on every question is still a default nobody chose."
  - "The bounce is decorative motion too, and the setting covers it. A small bounce still moves when the reader asked for less."
  - "Right. Motion should show a change, such as the result of a tap, and it must give way to words when the reader asks. An app can feel alive without moving everywhere."
  - "A change that answers a tap has a job, so it is worth keeping in a softer form. Removing it for everyone loses that job."
```
