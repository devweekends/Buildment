Maryam has fixed how Halaqa, her study-group app, looks and reads (a fictional app). Now she asks a classmate, Bashar, to open it on his own phone in the university library, where the connection is weak.

## A title and nothing else

<InteractiveCanvas slug="product-m3-scene" />

Bashar taps the Halaqa icon and sees the title "Study groups" with white space below it. He waits, taps the screen, waits again, and closes the app after 9 seconds. Switch to the second screen. It is no faster, and it only says what is happening.

<GoDeeper title="Go deeper: what Maryam saw over his shoulder">

Maryam kept quiet and watched. This is the habit from chapter 1.5: do not explain the app, watch what a person does. Bashar did three things in a row. He looked at the screen. He tapped it in case it was frozen. Then he closed the app and opened it again. The second time he waited 5 seconds and closed it again.

Afterwards he told her: "I thought it was broken. I did not know if there were no groups, or if my internet was bad." Maryam checked her server log that evening. The server had finished sending the list of groups about 12 seconds after he opened the app. He had closed it at 9 seconds. The data was on its way. The screen gave him no reason to wait.

Nothing was wrong with her search or her server. The weak part was the moment before the list arrived, a moment she had never designed. On her fast home connection it lasted a fraction of a second, so she had never seen it. Her classmates on weak data meet it often. In the library, the group list takes about 12 seconds to load.

</GoDeeper>

## One blank, three possible causes

<MermaidDiagram title="What a blank screen could mean, and what to say" chart={`flowchart TD
  B["Blank screen"] --> L["Still loading"]
  B --> E["Nothing to show yet"]
  B --> R["Something broke"]
  L --> LA["Say: wait, it is working"]
  E --> EA["Say: start one here"]
  R --> RA["Say: what failed, try again"]
`} />

Bashar could not tell these three apart. Each one needs a different message, and a blank screen gives none of them. This module is about moments like this one.

<GoDeeper title="Go deeper: why this matters for your career">

You will often be judged on a phone, on a slow connection, by a person who has seconds to spare. A reviewer of your project link may open it on a train or in a cafe with weak internet. If your project shows a blank screen, a reviewer cannot tell "slow network" from "broken", and may leave. Work that handles the moment when things are slow, empty or broken looks careful to a reviewer.

For you the unhappy moments are also the ones you can test alone. You do not need a user study to switch on flight mode, open your own app and look. A project built quickly, with or without an AI tool, may show only the happy screen, with data in it. A project that also draws the other screens tells a reviewer that you thought about real people.

</GoDeeper>
