```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Four facts about a fellow's library-room booking page (a fictional app). 1) On weak data the list of rooms takes about 6 seconds to arrive. 2) In those seconds the page shows a title and white space. 3) If no room is free, it shows 'No rooms'. 4) If the connection drops, it shows 'Something went wrong' and empties the form. What fits this module's advice for the first 6 seconds?"
options:
  - "A bar with the percentage done and the seconds left"
  - "The words 'No rooms' until the list arrives"
  - "A spinner with words such as 'Finding rooms...'"
  - "Nothing special; the list just appears when it is ready"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "A bar with a time is for waits over about 10 seconds, and fact 1 gives 6. At this length a spinner with words is enough."
  - "That is the empty state, and the list is not empty, only late. Loading comes first, then error, then empty."
  - "Right. For a wait of about 2 to 10 seconds, show that something is happening, and say so in words. The person then knows to keep waiting."
  - "Nothing special fits a wait under about a second. Six seconds is long enough for a person to think the page is broken."
```

  - "Keep the banner on screen for 10 seconds, not 3"
  - "Add a second banner that names the Phone number field"

  - "A thicker border is still a colour with no words. The tester still cannot tell what is wrong or how to fix it."
  - "A longer banner is still far from the field and still says nothing specific. The person must remember it while looking for the field."
  - "A second banner repeats the distance problem. The message belongs next to its field, not at the top of the screen."

What happens next?"

  - "Focus goes Name, Address, Phone, Pay, as the page shows"
  - "Focus lands on Phone before Address, then back up"
  - "Focus jumps to the Pay button before any field"
  - "Focus skips the Phone field and moves on to Address"

explanations:
  - "That is the order the page shows, but Tab follows the order in the page code, which is fact 2."
  - "Right. Tab follows the page code. Focus goes from Name to Phone, then to Address, then to Pay. The ring is visible, but the order jumps."
  - "Tab does not jump to a main button. It follows the order of the controls in the page code."
  - "Phone sits between Name and Address in the page code, so focus lands on it. No field is skipped just because of what it holds."

type: mcq
question: "An AI tool reviews Halaqa's study groups screen and writes: 'The message \"No groups yet\" is clear and friendly. Keep it exactly as it is, on every visit.' The screen loads slowly on library wifi. What is the mistake, and what is the right next check?"
options:
  - "The tone is too friendly; ask the tool for sterner wording"
  - "It should say Error; ask the tool for a red banner"
  - "There is no mistake; a clear message needs no check"
  - "It ignores loading; test it on a weak signal"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "The tone is not the issue. A sterner message would still show while the groups are on the way."
  - "Nothing failed, so Error would be a false alarm. The problem is the moment before the list arrives."
  - "An AI review is an opinion to check against a rule. The rule here is loading first, then error, then empty, and only a test shows what the screen says in the first seconds."
  - "Right. The review ignores the loading state. Open the screen on a weak signal and watch the first seconds to see whether it says there are no groups while they load."
```
