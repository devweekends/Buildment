type: mcq
question: "Four facts about Anwar's booking page for a sports club (a fictional app). 1) The list of bookings takes about 7 seconds to load on a weak phone. 2) At second 3 the page shows 'No bookings today'. 3) At second 7 five bookings appear. 4) When the connection drops, the page shows 'Something went wrong'. Which statement is correct?"
options:
  - "A bar with a time is needed at 7 seconds, because the wait is over 10 seconds"
  - "The loading words are not needed, because the list arrives in under a second"
  - "'No bookings today' at second 3 is false, because the bookings are still coming"
  - "'Something went wrong' is fine, because nobody knows why the connection dropped"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "Fact 1 says 7 seconds. The advice to say how long starts at about 10 seconds. At 7 seconds, a spinner with words is enough."
  - "Fact 1 says the list takes about 7 seconds, which is a real wait. A spinner with words, shown from about 2 seconds, tells the person that the app is working."
  - "Right. Facts 2 and 3 show that the page said 'none' while five bookings were on the way. Loading comes first, then error, then empty."
  - "Fact 4 says the connection dropped, so the cause is known. A good error names it, offers a step and keeps the person's input."

  - "It meets AA, but for a thumb it should grow to about 44 to 48"

  - "Space matters only for targets smaller than 24 by 24. Fact 1 says this one is 30 by 30, so its size alone meets AA."
  - "AA is only a floor. Fact 4 asks for an easy thumb target, and the advice for fingers is about 44 to 48."
  - "Right. At 30 by 30 it meets the AA floor of 24 by 24 by size alone. For a thumb, aim for about 44 to 48, which you can reach with padding."

options:
  - "None: a level AAA rule never applies to a real page, so the review is right"
  - "The pulse has no job and the setting matters; test with reduced motion on"
  - "The pulse colour is wrong; ask the tool for a calmer shade, then keep it"
  - "The word 'main' is wrong; ask the tool to name the action and trust it"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "AAA is above the course's bar, but the setting is a request from the person, and a pulse that loops beside other content can also fall under 2.2.2, a level A rule. The review is not right."
  - "Right. A loop that only attracts the eye has no job, and the person's setting should be honoured. The next check is to open the page with reduced motion on and see what still moves."
  - "The colour is not the issue. A calmer shade of a loop is still a loop with no job, and the tool is not the final judge of the fix."
  - "Naming the action does not touch the motion. An AI review is a set of opinions to check against a rule, not an answer to trust."
