## A ride app in one weekend

<GoDeeper title="What this module does">

Chapter 4.2 looks at what studies of AI coding tools show and do not show. It then lists what a reader can still trust on a project page. That is a real user, a measured result, a decision with its reason, and a log of how you used AI. Chapter 4.3 gives you four questions to test an idea, and a short path into open source for those who want it. Chapter 4.4 teaches you to plan with a time box, one user, one flow and one number.

In chapter 4.5 you write a one-page project plan. That plan is the part of your career kit that this module builds. You plan the project here. You do not need to build it to finish this course. Chapter 4.6 is a short quiz, and chapter 4.7 is the gate.

Module 5 uses your plan to decide what to show. Modules 6 and 8 point to it later. If you prefer not to use your own idea, you may use Areej's plan for a print shop, and say that you did.
