## Sixty applications, no replies

<InteractiveCanvas slug="career-m1-sixty-sent" />

Bashar is a backend engineer with two years at a small software agency. Over six weeks he sends 60 applications. No one replies. He worked hard, and nobody read it. The picture shows all the effort on one side and silence on the other. It does not tell us why.

<GoDeeper title="Meet Areej, the provided fellow">

Areej is a fictional fellow, and her numbers are illustrative. She is a full-stack developer in the final year of a software engineering degree, and she wants remote work with a small international team. You will meet her at every gate.

She comes with a full set of material: an old resume, an internship, freelance work, volunteer work and a study project. If you have no work yet, or you prefer not to use your own, you may use Areej's material for any task. Say that you did.

Nothing in this course asks you to share personal hardship. Use only what you choose to use.

</GoDeeper>

<GoDeeper title="Who to talk to if this feels heavy">

Looking closely at your own work can feel uncomfortable, especially when nobody has replied to your applications. If anything in this course feels heavy, or you are stuck, talk to the course's wellbeing contact. This is a named person, not a form or a bot, and you can reach them by phone.

Your instructors give you the name and the way to reach them. Your mentor is there for the work itself.

</GoDeeper>

<GoDeeper title="What this module does">

Chapter 1.2 shows why the first screen of a resume matters, and ends with the ten-second test. Chapter 1.3 lists what employers who publish their hiring say they look for, and where a certificate fits. Chapter 1.4 explains why smooth writing says less now that AI makes it cheap.

Chapter 1.5 is a short quiz. Chapter 1.6 is the gate: you test your own resume and record a baseline.

This module builds no new part of the kit. It builds the baseline that you compare with at the end of the course, in module 9.

If you do not have a LinkedIn profile yet, course 7, "Professional & Business Communication", covers the first steps. Come back here when you have one, or carry on and write "none yet" in the gate.

</GoDeeper>
