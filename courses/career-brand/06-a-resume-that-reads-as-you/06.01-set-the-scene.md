Waed is a fictional fellow, and her numbers are illustrative. Her story is made up, but the problem is real: a resume can be true in every line and still say nothing that only she could say.

## Lines that could be anyone's

<InteractiveCanvas slug="career-m6-lines-anyones" />

Waed is an operations coordinator on an NGO's logistics team. Her resume says "Responsible for coordinating shipments". An AI tool wrote her summary. Three other applicants send resumes with almost the same words. The reader opens all four and cannot tell them apart. Nothing on them is false. Nothing on them is hers alone.

<GoDeeper title="Why these lines are everywhere">

A line that names a duty was common long before AI tools. A duty is what the job asked for. Every person in the same job can write it, so it tells the reader very little about one person.

AI tools make this worse in one way: they make a smooth duty line cost almost nothing. Module 1 explained the result. When anyone can produce a smooth paragraph in a minute, a smooth paragraph proves less. What still proves something is a line that a reader can check, and that only one person could write.

This is not a reason to blame Waed, or the tool. She wrote down what her job was. She has not yet written down what changed because she did it.

</GoDeeper>

## The work that is missing

<InteractiveCanvas slug="career-m6-missing-tracker" />

Waed also built a shared tracker for her team. Missed delivery confirmations fell from about 30 a month to 6. She counted them from the team's own tracker, over three months before and three months after. None of this is on her resume. The result is real. It is only missing.

<GoDeeper title="How Waed counted">

Waed did not estimate her result from memory. She counted the missed delivery confirmations each month from the team's own tracker.

For the three months before, the count was about 30 a month. For the three months after, it was 6 a month. That is a fall of about 24 a month. Because the counts come from the team's tracker, a reader can ask to see them.

A good resume line keeps these parts: what changed, the numbers before and after, how long she counted, and where the numbers live. This module shows you how, one part at a time.

</GoDeeper>
