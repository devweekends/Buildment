```quiz
type: mcq
question: "In this made-up case, Omayma, a data analyst, drafts a claim card. Weekly report preparation fell from 90 to 30 minutes. The source is her timing sheet. Her share: she wrote the macro and her manager checked the output. What else changed: a new finance template arrived in the same period. What is missing from the card?"
options:
  - "The window, meaning which weeks the two times cover"
  - "The source, meaning where the two times were recorded"
  - "Her share, meaning what she did and what her manager did"
  - "What else changed, meaning other changes in the same weeks"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. The card has a source, her share and what else changed, but no dates. Without a window a reader cannot tell which weeks the 90 and the 30 minutes cover."
  - "The source is given: her timing sheet. A reader knows where to look, so nothing is missing here."
  - "Her share is stated: she wrote the macro and her manager checked the output. Nothing is missing here."
  - "What else changed is stated: a new finance template arrived in the same period. Nothing is missing here."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Ramzi, a mobile developer in this made-up case, writes a resume bullet: 'Cut crash rate from 3.1 to 1.2 percent (store console, 30 days before and after) by upgrading a networking library.' The team also reviewed the change. What is missing from the bullet?"
options:
  - "A window, meaning the dates that the two crash rates cover"
  - "A source, meaning where the two crash rates were read from"
  - "His own part, meaning what he did apart from the team"
  - "A before figure, meaning where the crash rate began"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "The bullet gives its window: 30 days before and after. Nothing is missing here."
  - "The bullet names its source: the store console. Nothing is missing here."
  - "Right. The bullet says what changed, but not whether Ramzi or his team upgraded the library. A reader cannot tell his part from the team's."
  - "The bullet gives its before figure: 3.1 percent. Nothing is missing here."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Salim, a full-stack developer in this made-up case, links his project from his resume. The link opens a demo that asks every visitor for an email sign-up. If the demo is down, nothing else exists. What is missing from his showcase?"
options:
  - "A longer README with install steps for every visitor who wants to run the project"
  - "A base layer with no sign-up: a case study page with screenshots and a short recording"
  - "A paid plan, so that the demo never sleeps and the sign-up page always loads in time"
  - "More features in the demo, so that each visitor has more to try once signed in"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "A longer README helps someone who already wants to run the project. It does not help a visitor who meets a sign-up wall, and it does nothing when the demo is down."
  - "Right. A case study page with screenshots and a short recording opens with no sign-up, and it still works when the demo is asleep."
  - "No step in this course needs a paid plan. A page that does not depend on the demo solves the problem for free."
  - "More features sit behind the same sign-up wall. A visitor who leaves at the wall never sees them."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Using what module 8 taught (checked 2026-10), look at this made-up case. Taleen, a frontend developer, posts a full copy of her article on another platform. Nothing in the copy says that the original lives on her own site. What is missing?"
options:
  - "A different title, so that search sees the copy as a separate article"
  - "More hashtags, so that readers can find the copy on the platform"
  - "A request that the platform hide the copy from search results"
  - "A line saying where it was first published, with a link home"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "A new title does not point anyone to the original. The copy needs to say where the original lives, not try to look like a new article."
  - "Hashtags help readers find the copy on that platform. They do not tell a reader, or a search, where the original lives."
  - "Hiding a copy does not send readers to the original, and a platform may not offer the choice. The fix is a line that points home."
  - "Right. A line such as 'First published at' her site, with a link, tells readers and search where the original lives."
```

type: mcq
question: "In this made-up case, Wesam, a backend developer, has a ledger row: the p95 of his orders search endpoint fell from 1.8 s to 0.6 s, over 14 days before and 14 days after, from the team dashboard. He added an index, and his lead reviewed it. An AI tool summarises his case study as 'Improved performance by 90 percent across the platform.' What is wrong with the summary?"
options:
  - "Only the percent is wrong, because the scope and the ownership both match the ledger row it came from"
  - "Nothing, because 90 percent is a fair rounding up of a drop of about two thirds in the time taken"
  - "It widens one endpoint to the platform, uses a figure the ledger lacks, and drops his lead's part"
  - "Only the word choice, because it should say we so that the whole team gets the credit it earned"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "The percent is wrong, but so is the scope: the ledger row covers one endpoint, not the platform. The summary also drops his lead's part."
  - "The drop was about two thirds, from 1.8 s to 0.6 s. Rounding it up to 90 percent is not rounding, and the summary still has no window or source."
  - "Right. The ledger row covers one endpoint's p95 over 14 days from the team dashboard, and his lead reviewed the change. The summary drops all of that, and 90 percent is not in the ledger."
  - "Saying we would hide Wesam's part further. The fix is to say what he did and what his lead did."
