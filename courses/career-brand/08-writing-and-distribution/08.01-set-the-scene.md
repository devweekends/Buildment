Diala is a fictional fellow. Her story is made up, but the problem is real: good work that a visitor cannot keep, find or believe. In this module you write one post about your work, share it, and plan a small personal site that holds your case study and your writing.

<LearningObjectives objectives={["Write a post about your own work (the problem, what you tried, the numbers and what you would change) and test it by asking someone to say it back", "Publish on a page you own first, then share copies in about ten places, each with a way back to the original", "Plan a small personal site with a case study and a blog, on a free static host, made yours by specifics only you have"]} />

## Her best post, gone

<InteractiveCanvas slug="career-m8-post-gone" />

Diala is a DevOps engineer. She wrote a strong post about cutting her team's CI pipeline from 22 minutes to 9. CI is the automatic build and test run after each change. The post lived on one blogging site. When she lost access to her account there, the post went too. The link on her resume now leads nowhere.

<GoDeeper title="What this module does">

Chapter 8.2 shows how to write a post about your own work in four parts, and how to test it by asking a person to say it back. Chapter 8.3 shows how to publish it once on a page you own and share it in about ten places, each with a way back to the original. Chapter 8.4 shows how to plan a small personal site: a few pages, a free static host, and specifics that make it yours.

Chapter 8.5 is where you write your post and your publishing plan. Chapter 8.6 is where you plan your website. Chapter 8.7 is a short quiz. Chapter 8.8 is the gate, where you record your say-it-back test and share your plans.

The piece of the kit this module adds is a post, a channel plan and a website plan. The case study you wrote in module 5 and the post you write here are the two things the site is built to hold.

</GoDeeper>

<GoDeeper title="Planning is required, going live is your choice">

You must plan the site and write the post. You do not have to put the site live. Some fellows have a laptop only when the power is on, or cannot reach a platform on their phone. A complete plan counts in full at the gate.

If you can put the site live, we strongly encourage it. A real address that opens on a stranger's phone is worth more than any plan. Chapter 8.4 shows a small first step so you find problems early.

</GoDeeper>

<GoDeeper title="If you write about AI-assisted work">

Course 13, "Learn and Build with AI", covers how to turn a week of commits into an article and how to say when AI helped. This module does not repeat that. It builds on it: your post gets one honest line about how you used AI, and every number in it is one you checked.

</GoDeeper>
