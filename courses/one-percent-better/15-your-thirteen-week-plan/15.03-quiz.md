```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Khalid starts his plan on Monday with all eleven habits, because 'Ramadan showed I can do it'. By Thursday he has missed four habits and feels like quitting. What helps first?"
options:
  - "Push through to the end of the week, then decide whether to continue"
  - "Stop for now, and start again next Ramadan, when it is easier"
  - "Keep all eleven, but post his progress so friends keep him going"
  - "Keep the habit he nearly did already, and add one new habit a week"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Pushing on with eleven at once is the plan that is already breaking. The size of the change is the problem."
  - "Waiting for Ramadan delays good deeds he can keep in small form today."
  - "Posting his progress invites the showing off module 1 warns about, and does not fix the size of the plan."
  - "Right. Take on only what you can manage, and the most consistent deeds are the most beloved, even if small (Sahih al-Bukhari 6465). One habit a week, attached to a steady one."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Sadaf catches Isha at the start of its time at home on most days, but she misses Fajr at its start four days a week. She sleeps around 0:30 after scrolling in bed. She wants to add al-Mulk before sleep next week. What helps first?"
options:
  - "Add al-Mulk next week, since it is the next habit on her list"
  - "Set three alarms for Fajr and keep her evenings as they are"
  - "Pray Fajr whenever she wakes, and stop tracking it for now"
  - "Phone away at bedtime, which serves al-Mulk and Fajr"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Her adding rule would say wait, since Fajr is below five of seven. Adding on top of a slipping habit makes both harder."
  - "Alarms do not change the short night. Fajr is won the night before (Sahih al-Bukhari 1142)."
  - "Praying as soon as she wakes is right on a missed morning (Sahih Muslim 684), but it is not the plan to stop missing."
  - "Right. The phone away and an earlier night protect both the night recitations and Fajr (modules 2 and 6). One change helps two habits."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Ammar's friends ask how he learned the morning adhkar. He feels the pull to show them his tracker, which has six weeks without a miss. What helps first?"
options:
  - "Explain the method, a card and a daily walk, and offer cards"
  - "Show them the tracker, since it proves the method works"
  - "Say nothing, since any talk about his worship may be showing off"
  - "Tell them he has not missed for six weeks, but not show the tracker"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. Share the idea, not the score (Sahih Muslim 1893), and keep the record private."
  - "The tracker is his score. They need the method, not his record."
  - "Saying nothing loses a chance to guide them to good, which has its own reward (Sahih Muslim 1893)."
  - "Telling them his streak is still sharing the score."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Tanveer reads a page after Fajr and says the adhkar after every prayer. He travels for five days and does almost none of it. At home again, he plans to 'repay' with five pages a day for a week. What helps first?"
options:
  - "Repay as planned, so nothing he missed is lost"
  - "Wait for the start of next month and restart the plan from week one"
  - "His usual page and adhkar from the next cue, with no debt"
  - "Drop the Quran page, since travel shows it is too hard"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "A growing debt makes the restart heavy. And his usual deeds were written for him while travel stopped him (Sahih al-Bukhari 2996)."
  - "Waiting turns five days into weeks. The next cue is tomorrow's Fajr."
  - "Right. Restart at the next cue at the usual size. His habits were steady before the journey, so they come back quickly."
  - "One journey does not show the habit is too hard. It was steady for weeks before."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "In her Friday review, Zara sees that the adhkar after Asr were 2 of 7 at work. Her notes say 'meeting' five times. She also forwarded a message about the 'secret reward' of these adhkar, with no source. What helps first?"
options:
  - "Drop the adhkar after Asr, since work does not allow them"
  - "A walking version after Asr, and share only sourced hadith"
  - "Change her anchor, her set and her time all together this week"
  - "Add a new habit next week, to balance the one that slipped"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "There is a version that fits her day. Allah praises those who remember Him standing, sitting and lying down (Quran 3:191)."
  - "Right. One change aimed at the cause in her notes (module 15), and the habit from module 6 of checking a saying before passing it on."
  - "Three changes at once will not show which one helped."
  - "Her adding rule would say wait while one habit is at 2 of 7."
```
