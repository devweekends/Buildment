```quiz
question: "Fahad wants one short dua to say every time he leaves the washroom. Which one is sound?"
options:
  - "Bismillahi tawakkaltu 'alallah (Sunan Abi Dawud, no. 5095)"
  - "Ghufranak, I ask Your forgiveness (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 7)"
  - "Allahumma iftah li abwaba rahmatik (Sahih Muslim 713)"
  - "Alhamdulillahilladhi ahyana (Sahih al-Bukhari 6312)"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "That is the dua for leaving the house."
  - "Right. When the Prophet, peace be upon him, came out of the washroom he said: Ghufranak (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 7, sahih)."
  - "That is the dua for entering the mosque."
  - "That is the dua on waking."
```

```quiz
explanations:
```
