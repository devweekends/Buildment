```quiz
type: mcq
question: "At dinner, a relative says something true but embarrassing about a cousin who is not there. What is it, according to Sahih Muslim 2589?"
options:
  - "Not backbiting, because it is true"
  - "Backbiting, because the cousin would dislike it"
  - "Slander, because the cousin is absent"
  - "Allowed, because it is said only among close family"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "The Prophet said that if it is true, it is backbiting; if false, it is slander (Sahih Muslim 2589)."
  - "Right. Mentioning your brother in a way he dislikes is backbiting, even if true (Sahih Muslim 2589)."
  - "Slander is saying something false. This is true, so it is backbiting."
  - "Being among family does not change what backbiting is."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Laiba's eyes fall on an indecent image in a shop window. What is the habit taught in module 10?"
options:
  - "Look again to make sure of what it was"
  - "Feel guilty for the first glance for the rest of the day"
  - "Complain loudly to the shop owner"
  - "Turn her eyes away at once, without a second look"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "The second look is what the eyes are responsible for (Sahih Muslim 2657)."
  - "The first unintended glance is not the sin; the Prophet told Jarir to turn away (Sahih Muslim 2159)."
  - "Complaining is not required; turning away is the habit."
  - "Right. Turn away from the sudden glance (Sahih Muslim 2159); lower the gaze (Quran 24:31)."
```
