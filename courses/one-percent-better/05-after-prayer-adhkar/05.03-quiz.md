```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Javed asks what the Prophet, peace be upon him, said first when he finished his prayer, according to Sahih Muslim 591. Which answer is right?"
options:
  - "He stood up straight away and turned to his work"
  - "He said SubhanAllah 33 times, before anything else"
  - "He asked forgiveness three times, then 'Allahumma antas-salam'"
  - "He said 'Bismillahilladhi...' three times, before anything else"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "That is not what Sahih Muslim 591 describes. It describes words he said as soon as he finished."
  - "The tasbih is from Sahih Muslim 597. Sahih Muslim 591 describes forgiveness first."
  - "Right. Thawban reported that he asked forgiveness three times and said: O Allah, You are Peace, and from You comes peace (Sahih Muslim 591)."
  - "That is a morning and evening dhikr (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3388), not what Sahih Muslim 591 describes after the prayer."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Noman says the 33, 33, 33 after each prayer but stops at ninety-nine. A friend says there is one more phrase in Sahih Muslim 597. What completes the hundred?"
options:
  - "Astaghfirullah, once more"
  - "Allahu akbar, a thirty-fourth time"
  - "Allahumma a'inni 'ala dhikrika wa shukrika..."
  - "La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah..."
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "The istighfar comes at the start (Sahih Muslim 591), not at the hundred."
  - "That is a different practice. Sahih Muslim 597 completes the hundred with the tahlil."
  - "That is Mu'adh's dua (Sunan Abi Dawud 1522), said after the hundred."
  - "Right. The hundred is completed with: there is no god but Allah, alone, without partner; His is the dominion and the praise, and He has power over all things (Sahih Muslim 597)."
```

```quiz

options:
  - "Skip them until her baby is older and she has more time to sit"
  - "Say them while she picks up and settles the baby"
  - "Say them all later in the day, together, when the baby sleeps"
  - "Ask someone to hold the baby so she can always sit for them"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "There is no need to wait. A version that fits her day keeps the habit going."
  - "Right. Allah praises those who remember Him standing, sitting and lying down (Quran 3:191). The words can be said while she cares for her baby."
  - "The adhkar belong after each prayer. Saving them for later loses the cue."
  - "That could help sometimes, but it is not needed for the habit to count."
```
