```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Yasir hears the iqamah for Maghrib as he reaches the mosque gate. He can hear that the imam has already said the first takbir. His friend beside him starts to run. What does Sahih al-Bukhari 636 tell Yasir to do?"
options:
  - "Run with his friend, so that he still catches the first rak'ah in full"
  - "Wait outside and pray alone after the congregation has finished"
  - "Walk in calmly, join where the imam is, and complete what he missed"
  - "Go home and pray there, since the first takbir is already lost"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "The Prophet, peace be upon him, told us to walk to the prayer with calm and dignity and not rush (Sahih al-Bukhari 636)."
  - "Praying alone loses the congregation he can still join. The hadith tells him to join and complete."
  - "Right. Pray what you catch and complete what you missed (Sahih al-Bukhari 636). He still prays with the congregation."
  - "Missing the first takbir does not mean missing the congregation. Joining late is still praying with it."
```

  - "A prayer in its last minutes is valid, but this module's habit is the start of the time."
  - "Joining prayers is a fiqh question with conditions, for a scholar, not a time-saving habit."
