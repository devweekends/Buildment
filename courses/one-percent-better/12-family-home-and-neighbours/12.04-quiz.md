```quiz
  - "Your children first, then your parents after them, then your siblings"
  - "Your mother, your mother, your mother, then your father"

explanations:

  - "The question was about who most deserves good company, and the answer named the mother."
```

```quiz
options:
  - "Wait for Eid, when the whole family visits her anyway every year"
  - "A weekly call to one relative from a list, starting with her"
  - "Send her a forwarded message on Fridays"
  - "Ask his mother to pass on his salam"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "Waiting months more keeps the tie weak."
  - "Right. Whoever wants his provision increased and life extended, let him keep ties of kinship (Sahih al-Bukhari 5986)."
  - "A forward is not the same as speaking with her."
  - "Passing salam is kind, but a call keeps the tie himself."
```
