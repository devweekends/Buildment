```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Ghada built an order form page for a bakery. Her browser shows these parts of the first view: JavaScript 140 KB, images 60 KB, fonts 20 KB, style rules 10 KB, page text 10 KB. Her starting limit is about 170 KB, and the bakery's customers use phones on weak connections. Which conclusion do the facts support?"
options:
  - "Within the limit, since the images are only 60 KB"
  - "70 KB over, so start with the 140 KB of JavaScript"
  - "70 KB over, so start by cutting the 10 KB of page text"
  - "40 KB over, so a small change to one image fixes it"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "The parts add up to 240 KB, not 60 KB. The images are only one part of the page, so the page is over the limit."
  - "Right. 140 plus 60 plus 20 plus 10 plus 10 is 240, which is 70 over 170. Only the JavaScript is bigger than 70 KB, so it is the part that can close the gap."
  - "Page text is only 10 KB. Removing all of it would save 10 KB, far short of the 70 KB gap."
  - "240 minus 170 is 70, not 40. The images are 60 KB in total, so a small change to one image cannot save 70."
```

  - "Because of the agreeing habit, the same assistant tends to say yes. A book is real only if a listing outside the chat shows it."
  - "A pasted plan holds only the assistant's words, and the book may not exist. Nothing has been checked yet."

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Wisam is hired by a cafe owner to make a 30-second ad. Facts: the owner's voice would suit the ad. The owner is happy to help, but nobody has told him how the voice would be used. The ad will run on a platform that labels realistic AI audio. Wisam's voice-tool account is free. Which next step do the facts support?"
options:
  - "Clone the voice from the owner's old video on the free account"
  - "Pay for a cloning plan, and clone the voice after the owner nods"
  - "Use a stock voice with no mention that it is an AI voice"
  - "Ask the owner to record the script, and tell him where it will run"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "A free account cannot clone a voice, and the owner has not been told the use. Two facts block this step."
  - "A nod is not explicit, informed consent, because the owner has not been told how the voice would be used. Paying for the plan does not fix that."
  - "The platform labels realistic AI audio, so a stock voice needs the words AI voice on the ad. Leaving them out breaks that fact."
  - "Right. A recording by the owner is his real voice, so there is no clone and no cloning plan is needed. Telling him where it will run keeps his yes informed."
```

  - "Check the compute item and delete what he does not use"
  - "Upgrade the plan, because $17 is already over the budget"

explanations:
  - "A budget alert tells him. It does not stop spending, so costs can pass $20 with no pause."
  - "Alerts can lag behind charges, so the cost may pass the limit before the alert arrives. Waiting is not a plan."
  - "Right. The costs so far are 11.50 plus 4.20 plus 1.30 plus 0, which is $17. The forecast of $26 is over the $20 alert. Compute is the largest item, so he starts there."
  - "$17 is below the $20 alert, not above it. Upgrading would not lower the forecast either."
