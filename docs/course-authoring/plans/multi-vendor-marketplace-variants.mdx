# Multi-vendor marketplace: variants and combinations

The FreshMarket course (`content/import/multi-vendor-marketplace/`, about 240 hours) is the general
course. This plan proposes six variants so that two fellows who finish different variants leave
with different portfolio projects, plus specializations that can be combined with any variant.

This is a roadmap for instructors to confirm, not course content. No variant chapter is written
until the instructors approve the variant (see the variant rules in `agent-ip/`, Aqib's course
factory, and [`../tracks.mdx`](../tracks.mdx)).

## What stays the same in every variant

The spine and outcomes do not change, so every variant can be assessed the same way:

- product scoping and actors, data modelling, authentication and authorization with vendor
  isolation;
- seller onboarding and a seller dashboard, a public catalogue with search and filters;
- a cart or booking that spans several sellers and splits into per-seller orders at checkout;
- payments in Stripe test mode, with webhooks;
- media uploads, caching, background jobs and queues;
- tests, deployment over HTTPS, and a defended architecture walkthrough;
- the same stack (MongoDB, Express, React, Node, Redis, BullMQ, Cloudinary, Stripe) and the same
  time budget (210 to 280 hours).

## The six variants

Each variant changes at least four of the six dimensions in the uniqueness contract: primary user
journey, hardest constraint, data model, hero feature, visual and verbal identity, and capstone
evidence.

| Variant | Primary journey | Hardest constraint | Hero feature | Data-model change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Craftly**: handmade goods | Buyer requests a custom piece, maker quotes, buyer pays | One-of-a-kind stock and made-to-order lead times | Custom request and quote flow | `CustomRequest`, `Quote`, `ProductionSlot` |
| **GigBay**: freelance services | Buyer posts a brief, sellers offer, work ships in milestones | Paying per milestone with held funds and approval | Milestone orders with delivery approval | `Gig`, `Milestone`, `Delivery`, `Dispute` |
| **KitShare**: equipment rental | Renter picks dates, owner confirms, item returns | Date-range availability with no double bookings | Availability calendar with holds and deposits | `Listing`, `Booking` (date range), `Deposit`, `ConditionReport` |
| **HomeKitchen**: home-cook meals | Customer orders for a time slot in their area | Kitchen capacity per slot and delivery zones | Slot ordering with live capacity | `Kitchen`, `MenuItem` options, `DeliverySlot`, `Zone` |
| **BookSwap**: student textbook exchange | Student lists a used book or a want, gets matched | Matching editions and condition, campus pickup | ISBN lookup and want-list matching | `Edition` (ISBN), `Copy` (condition), `WantList`, `PickupPoint` |
| **TutorHub**: tutoring lessons | Learner books recurring lessons with a tutor | Recurring sessions across time zones | Time-zone-aware scheduling and lesson packages | `Tutor`, `AvailabilityRule`, `LessonPackage`, `Session` |

### Capstone evidence per variant

| Variant | What the fellow demos and defends |
|---|---|
| Craftly | A custom order from request to quote to paid order, and how lead times are shown honestly |
| GigBay | A two-milestone job paid, delivered, approved, and one milestone disputed |
| KitShare | Two renters trying the same dates, one blocked, and a deposit returned after a condition report |
| HomeKitchen | A slot filling up under load, and orders rejected outside the delivery zone |
| BookSwap | A want-list match by ISBN and condition, and a pickup arranged at a campus point |
| TutorHub | A weekly lesson series booked across two time zones, including a daylight saving change |

## Combinations

A combination is one variant plus optional layers. The layers add depth and personal interest
without changing the outcomes.

| Axis | Options | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Industry or use case | The six variants above; new ones need the uniqueness check | Domain, journey, constraint, data model, hero feature |
| Design elements | Product name, palette, tone of voice, seed content, demo story | Visual and verbal identity, so projects look different in a portfolio |
| Specialization (stretch) | Payments depth, search and recommendations, realtime order tracking, offline-friendly mobile web, AI-assisted listings, observability | One extra module of stretch hours, with its own evidence |

Rules for combinations:

- A specialization adds a stretch module; it never replaces a core module.
- A combination must still fit the time budget: the core path stays within 210 hours.
- Offline-friendly mobile web is a good default suggestion for fellows who learn on phones with
  unreliable connections, because it is a skill they can demonstrate from their own situation.
- A combination of design elements alone is not a variant: names and colours on the same product
  are a reskin.

## Open decisions for instructors

1. Are these six the right domains for our fellows, or should one be replaced with an NGO or
   local-economy use case?
2. Should every fellow pick a variant, or do some take FreshMarket itself?
3. Which specializations do we have mentors for today?
4. Is the TutorHub time-zone constraint too hard for the core path, or should it be its stretch
   module?
5. Who are the two instructors who confirm each variant roadmap?

## Next steps after confirmation

For each confirmed variant: write its brief and full roadmap, have it confirmed, then derive the
chapters from the general course module by module, changing the scenarios, data model, hero
feature and capstone. Each variant then goes through the same validation, audit, import and
click-through as any course in [`../README.mdx`](../README.mdx).
