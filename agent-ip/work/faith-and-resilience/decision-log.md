## Risks

| # | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Drafting and critic A share a model family | Critic B and the blind key check run on a second model family; a human key check, instructor sign-off and the scholar's review are requested in the PR |
| R2 | A verse, hadith, grading or Arabic text is wrong | Only the dossier's checked references; Arabic compared by script with quran.com and sunnah.com; critic B checks chapters against the dossier; the scholar checks every item before publishing |
| R3 | Content hurts a grieving learner (preachy, shaming, "your loss is good for you") | Sensitive-areas rules in the writer pack; critic B scores tone and safety; a named human contact in every hardship chapter |
| R4 | Chapters drafted before G2B and before the scholar's review may need rework | Short roadmap with open decisions; the PR states the order of work |
| R5 | 1,200-word chapters are long for second-language readers on phones | Short paragraphs, worked cases, interactions spaced through the text |
