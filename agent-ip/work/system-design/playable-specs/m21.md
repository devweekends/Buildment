# Module 21 playable specs (news feed and timelines)

Four reserved slugs. The builder creates each folder (`frontend/src/canvas/<slug>/`), registers it and writes its `spec.md`. All four sit on `BeatFrame` (`primitives/beat-frame.tsx`), draw on a 420 wide viewBox with labels of at least 11.5 units, use `SvgNode` cards and `VerdictBanner`, take theme tokens only, and keep every verdict at 38 characters or fewer. At most two controls sit outside the picture, each a 44px target with a visible label; every other input is inside the picture. The nine watch-only scenes of the module (`sysdesign-m21-*`, kind `scene`) already exist as data in `frontend/src/canvas/`. They use the same names (Hikaya, Dina, Karim, Feed, Timelines) and the same numbers as the playables below, so the picture and the play agree.

Every number below was computed with code (the formulas are short and shown). All models are deterministic; none uses a random number at play time. Course numbers shared by all four, defined in chapter 21.1: 100 million accounts, 50 million daily users; 10 feed opens per daily user per day; 0.2 posts per daily user per day; 150 follows per account on average; peak 3 times the average; a day is 86,400 seconds; a timeline holds 800 post ids of 8 bytes. Defined in chapter 21.3: the fan-out fleet is planned for 150,000 timeline writes a second (half of a 300,000 maximum, chapter 2.5); a lookup is slow 1% of the time, independently, so a feed open with n lookups meets at least one slow one with probability 1 - 0.99^n ([S8], chapter 2.4).

## sysdesign-m21-estimate-play

- **Chapter and beat:** 21.1 beat 2, "Drag the volume, find the driver".
- **Claim:** For a feed, posts are few and reading is the load: feed opens a second dwarf posts a second, and building each feed from scratch would cost one lookup per followed account. The card with the biggest consequence is ringed.
- **Primitives:** `estimate-chain.tsx` (cards joined by links, some cards are inputs: tap, then drag sideways or use arrow keys), `VerdictBanner`. No toggle.
- **Input:** three draggable cards. Daily users: 10M, 25M, 50M, 100M (default 50M). Feed opens per user per day: 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 (default 10). Follows per account: 50, 150, 500 (default 150). The peak factor (3), the posts per daily user (0.2), the ids per timeline (800) and the bytes per id (8) are fixed cards labelled "assumption".
- **Model and formulas:**
  - Feed opens a day = daily users x opens per user.
  - Feed opens a second, average = opens a day / 86,400. Peak = average x 3.
  - Posts a day = daily users x 0.2. Posts a second, average = posts a day / 86,400. Peak = average x 3.
  - Lookups a second if each open builds its own list = peak feed opens x follows.
  - Timeline storage = daily users x 800 x 8 bytes.
  - Reads per post = opens per user / 0.2.
- **Picture:** three chains that start at the Daily users card. Chain 1: Daily users > (x opens) Feed opens a day > (divide by 86,400 s) Feed opens a second, average > (x 3 peak) Feed opens a second, peak > (x follows) Lookups a second if built on read. Chain 2: Daily users > (x 0.2) Posts a day > (divide by 86,400 s) Posts a second, average > (x 3 peak) Posts a second, peak. Chain 3: Daily users > (x 800 x 8 B) Timeline storage. Dots flow along the links at a speed set by the value. Format: `17,361 /s`, `2.6M /s`, `347 /s`, `320 GB`. Ring (tone warn) on the Lookups card when it is 100,000 a second or more, otherwise on the peak feed opens card when reads per post are 25 or more, otherwise none.
- **Numbers to check** (computed with the formulas above):

  | Daily users | Opens | Follows | Feed opens /s peak | Posts /s peak | Lookups /s if built on read | Timeline storage | Reads per post |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 50M | 10 | 150 | 17,361 | 347 | 2.60M | 320 GB | 50 |
  | 10M | 10 | 150 | 3,472 | 69 | 521,000 | 64 GB | 50 |
  | 25M | 10 | 150 | 8,681 | 174 | 1.30M | 160 GB | 50 |
  | 100M | 10 | 150 | 34,722 | 694 | 5.21M | 640 GB | 50 |
  | 50M | 2 | 150 | 3,472 | 347 | 521,000 | 320 GB | 10 |
  | 50M | 1 | 150 | 1,736 | 347 | 260,000 | 320 GB | 5 |
  | 50M | 5 | 150 | 8,681 | 347 | 1.30M | 320 GB | 25 |
  | 50M | 20 | 150 | 34,722 | 347 | 5.21M | 320 GB | 100 |
  | 50M | 10 | 50 | 17,361 | 347 | 868,000 | 320 GB | 50 |
  | 50M | 10 | 500 | 17,361 | 347 | 8.68M | 320 GB | 50 |
  | 10M | 1 | 50 | 347 | 69 | 17,400 | 64 GB | 5 |

- **Verdicts:**
  - `Reads dwarf posts: build ahead` (reads per post 25 or more; warn)
  - `Reads lead: building ahead pays` (reads per post 10 to 24; warn)
  - `Close: building on read could work` (reads per post under 10; good)
- **Reduced motion:** the dots do not flow; the cards show their final values.
- **Accessibility:** each input card is a `role="slider"` with value text such as "50 million daily users, 17,361 feed opens a second at peak, 347 posts a second, 2.6 million lookups a second if built on read"; the ring is also written as text in the verdict.
- **Sources:** Arithmetic on course assumptions (chapter 2.3 for the method). No external source.

## sysdesign-m21-action-map

- **Chapter and beat:** 21.2 beat 5, "Tap an action, see what it touches".
- **Claim:** Each action in Hikaya touches a different set of blocks at a different rate. Opening the feed touches few blocks but very often; publishing touches the most blocks but least often, so the heavy work belongs on the write path where a queue can absorb bursts.
- **Primitives:** `block-map.tsx` (blocks, links, a lit path and a pill on each block), with a row of four action chips inside the picture replacing "tap a block" as the selector. If the primitive cannot take actions, compose `SvgNode` cards and `link-line.tsx` the same way. `VerdictBanner`. No toggle.
- **Input:** tap one of four action chips: Open feed, Open profile, Publish a post, Follow. Default: Open feed. Arrow keys move between chips.
- **Blocks (eight, fixed layout):** Gateway, Feed service, Post service, Timeline cache + store, Post cache + store, Follow store, Queue, Fan-out workers. Links: Gateway to Feed service and Post service; Feed service to Timeline and Post stores; Post service to Post store and Queue; Queue to Fan-out workers; Fan-out workers to Follow store and Timeline store.
- **Model:** peak rates from the 21.1 numbers (50M daily users, x 3 peak) with two more course assumptions: 5 profile views and 0.1 new follows per daily user per day.
  - Open feed = 50,000,000 x 10 / 86,400 x 3 = 17,361 a second.
  - Open profile = 50,000,000 x 5 / 86,400 x 3 = 8,681 a second.
  - Publish = 50,000,000 x 0.2 / 86,400 x 3 = 347 a second.
  - Follow = 50,000,000 x 0.1 / 86,400 x 3 = 174 a second, two writes each (one per copy) = 347 writes a second.
- **Pills per action** (value on each touched block; untouched blocks are dimmed with no pill):

  | Action | Rate | Gateway | Feed service | Post service | Timelines | Post store | Follow store | Queue | Fan-out workers |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Open feed | 17,361 | 17,361 | 17,361 | none | 17,361 list reads | 17,361 batch reads (347,000 posts) | none | none | none |
  | Open profile | 8,681 | 8,681 | none | 8,681 | none | 8,681 reads (author index) | none | none | none |
  | Publish a post | 347 | 347 | none | 347 | 52,000 writes if 150 followers each (more in 21.3) | 347 writes | 347 follower lookups | 347 events | 347 jobs |
  | Follow | 174 | 174 | none | none | none | none | 347 writes (two copies) | none | none |

- **Picture:** blocks as `SvgNode` cards in two columns (read side left, write side right). The chosen action chip is filled. Touched blocks light up in the accent tone with their pill; the request travels as dots along the touched links at a speed that grows with the rate (three speeds only: under 1,000, 1,000 to 10,000, over 10,000). The Timelines pill for Publish uses tone warn and has a small "21.3" tag.
- **Verdicts:**
  - `Open feed: few blocks, very often` (good)
  - `Open profile: one read path` (good)
  - `Publish: many blocks, 350 a second` (warn)
  - `Follow: two small writes` (good)
- **Reduced motion:** dots become arrows with the rate printed on them.
- **Accessibility:** action chips are buttons with visible labels; each pill is also read out as text such as "Timelines: 17,361 list reads a second".
- **Sources:** Arithmetic on course assumptions (chapter 21.2 fold).

## sysdesign-m21-fanout-play

- **Chapter and beat:** 21.3 beat 5, "Drag the line between push and pull".
- **Claim:** Both extremes fail. Pushing every account needs twice the planned write capacity because a few huge accounts cause most writes. Pulling every account makes most feed opens meet a slow lookup. Only a line between them keeps both within limits, and it sits where the follower counts jump.
- **Primitives:** `snap-stops-rail.tsx` or `picture-slider.tsx` for the line (a draggable vertical line across five group cards, inside the picture), two horizontal gauge bars (`svg-stat.tsx` or a small custom bar), `VerdictBanner`. No toggle.
- **Input:** the line has six snap positions, from left to right: "push everyone" (nobody pulled), then pulled from Celebrity, from Big, from Popular, from Local, and "pull everyone". Default: push everyone. Arrow keys step one position.
- **Groups** (a made-up composite, chapter 21.3 fold; columns: accounts, followers each, posts a day each, edges = accounts x followers, writes a day if pushed = accounts x posts x followers):

  | Group | Accounts | Followers each | Posts a day each | Edges | Writes a day if pushed | Follows per reader (edges / 100M) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Everyday | 80,000,000 | 100 | 0.11 | 8.0 B | 0.88 B | 80 |
  | Local | 4,000,000 | 1,000 | 0.25 | 4.0 B | 1.00 B | 40 |
  | Popular | 100,000 | 10,000 | 1 | 1.0 B | 1.00 B | 10 |
  | Big | 10,000 | 100,000 | 2 | 1.0 B | 2.00 B | 10 |
  | Celebrity | 1,000 | 1,000,000 | 4 | 1.0 B | 4.00 B | 10 |
  | Total | | | about 9.9 million posts a day | 15.0 B (150 per account) | 8.88 B | 150 |

- **Model and formulas:** let P = the set of pulled groups (those at or right of the line).
  - Timeline writes a second at peak = (sum of "writes a day if pushed" over groups not in P) / 86,400 x 3.
  - Share of plan = writes a second at peak / 150,000.
  - Lookups per feed open = 1 + sum of "follows per reader" over groups in P.
  - Share of feed opens that meet a slow lookup = 1 - 0.99^(lookups).
  - Limits: writes at most 150,000 a second (100% of plan); slow share at most 20%.
  - Caption under the picture: the slowest post to land (the largest pushed account), in seconds at 150,000 writes a second = largest pushed followers / 150,000: 20,000,000 / 150,000 = 133 s when nobody is pulled (the 20 million account is the largest in Celebrity); 100,000 / 150,000 = 0.7 s when Celebrity is pulled; 10,000 / 150,000 = 0.07 s when Big and Celebrity are pulled; below that, under 0.01 s.
- **Picture:** five group cards in a row on a log axis (100, 1,000, 10,000, 100,000, 1,000,000 followers), each with its account count. The line stands between two cards; cards on the right are tinted "pulled", cards on the left "pushed". Under the cards: a write gauge (bar with a marker at 150,000 and the value and percent), and a read gauge (bar with a marker at 20% and the value and the lookups per open). A bar turns warn/bad when over its limit and good when within.
- **Numbers to check:**

  | Line (pulled from) | Writes /s at peak | Share of plan | Lookups per open | Opens meeting a slow lookup | Slowest post |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Nobody (push all) | 308,333 | 206% | 1 | 1.0% | 133 s |
  | Celebrity | 169,444 | 113% | 11 | 10.5% | 0.7 s |
  | Big | 100,000 | 67% | 21 | 19.0% | 0.07 s |
  | Popular | 65,278 | 44% | 31 | 26.8% | under 0.01 s |
  | Local | 30,556 | 20% | 71 | 51.0% | under 0.01 s |
  | Everyone (pull all) | 0 | 0% | 151 | 78.1% | none |

- **Verdicts:**
  - `Writes too heavy: pull more accounts` (writes over 100% of plan and slow share within the limit; bad)
  - `Reads too slow: push more accounts` (slow share over 20% and writes within plan; bad)
  - `Both over: widen the budget` (both over; bad; not reachable with these groups, keep for a changed model)
  - `Both within limits: the hybrid works` (good; only the "Big" position)
- **Reduced motion:** gauges and tints update at once, no sliding.
- **Accessibility:** the line is a `role="slider"` with value text such as "pull accounts with 100,000 followers or more: 100,000 writes a second, 67 percent of plan, 21 lookups per feed open, 19 percent of feed opens meet a slow lookup".
- **Sources:** Arithmetic on course assumptions (chapter 21.3 fold). The slow-lookup probability 1 - 0.99^n is the tail-at-scale arithmetic [S8]. The groups are a made-up composite and no company's graph is claimed [S103].

## sysdesign-m21-rank-play

- **Chapter and beat:** 21.4 beat 2, "Drag how much age counts".
- **Claim:** A ranking score mixes closeness, popularity and age. How fast age counts decides who leads: a short half-life puts fresh posts from close friends first, a long one lets old popular posts climb.
- **Primitives:** `sorter.tsx` as the base (rows that reorder with a transition), `snap-stops-rail.tsx` for the half-life rail inside the picture, `VerdictBanner`. No toggle.
- **Input:** half-life on a rail with snap points 1, 3, 6, 12, 24 and 48 hours, default 6. Arrow keys step one snap.
- **Model and formulas:** score = closeness x (1 + ln(1 + likes)) x 0.5^(age in hours / half-life in hours). Closeness runs from 0 to 1. `ln` is the natural logarithm. The five posts (made up):

  | Post | Closeness | Likes | Age | Engagement term 1 + ln(1 + likes) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Dina (close friend) | 1.0 | 6 | 1 h | 2.95 |
  | Karim (star) | 0.5 | 400,000 | 20 h | 13.90 |
  | Rafa Bakery | 0.3 | 40 | 5 h | 4.71 |
  | Omar (cousin) | 0.8 | 3 | 9 h | 2.39 |
  | Daily News | 0.4 | 2,000 | 3 h | 8.60 |

- **Picture:** five post cards stacked in rank order, each showing author, a closeness chip (0.3 to 1.0), likes, age, and a score bar on the right. When the half-life changes the cards slide to their new places (CSS transition, 300 ms). Above the stack, the formula as three chips joined by multiplication signs. Tapping a card shows its three factors as a small breakdown.
- **Numbers to check** (score of each post, best first):

  | Half-life | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 h | Dina 1.47 | News 0.43 | Bakery 0.04 | Omar 0.00 | Karim 0.00 |
  | 3 h | Dina 2.34 | News 1.72 | Bakery 0.45 | Omar 0.24 | Karim 0.07 |
  | 6 h | Dina 2.62 | News 2.43 | Bakery 0.79 | Karim 0.69 | Omar 0.67 |
  | 12 h | News 2.89 | Dina 2.78 | Karim 2.19 | Omar 1.14 | Bakery 1.06 |
  | 24 h | Karim 3.90 | News 3.16 | Dina 2.86 | Omar 1.47 | Bakery 1.22 |
  | 48 h | Karim 5.21 | News 3.29 | Dina 2.90 | Omar 1.68 | Bakery 1.32 |

- **Verdicts:**
  - `Fresh friends lead` (top post is Dina; good)
  - `Popular recent news leads` (top post is Daily News; warn)
  - `Old but popular posts climb` (top post is Karim; warn)
- **Reduced motion:** cards jump to their new places with no slide.
- **Accessibility:** the rail is a `role="slider"` with value text such as "half-life 12 hours: Daily News first, then Dina, Karim, Omar, Bakery"; the order is also available as a list read by a screen reader.
- **Sources:** A course formula for teaching. Real rankers use trained models; the course makes no claim about any company's ranker (chapter 21.4 source note).
