### sysdesign-m14-2pc-crash (14.2 beat "Crash the coordinator at each step")
- **Claim:** In two-phase commit, a database that has voted yes cannot decide alone. If the coordinator crashes after the votes, both databases wait with their locks held, and the orders behind them queue for as long as the coordinator is down [S77].
- **Model:** One coordinator and two participants, Stock DB (A) and Payments DB (B). The protocol has five stopping points. The learner picks where the coordinator crashes and how long it stays down. Every order needs rows in both databases, and orders arrive at a constant 2 per second.
- **Crash points and states:**

  | Point | What happened before the crash | A | B | Blocked? |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0 | Nothing sent | Working | Working | No |
  | 1 | Both voted yes, decision not logged | Prepared | Prepared | A and B |
  | 2 | Decision (commit) logged, nothing sent | Prepared | Prepared | A and B |
  | 3 | Commit sent to A only | Committed | Prepared | B only (it can ask A) |
  | 4 | Commit sent to both | Committed | Committed | No |

- **Formulas:**
  - Orders waiting = 2 orders per second x downtime in seconds, when at least one database is prepared (points 1, 2 and 3). Otherwise 0.
  - Locks are held for the whole downtime. At point 0 and point 4 the hold time is 0 extra seconds.
- **Check points (2 orders per second):**

  | Downtime | Orders waiting (points 1 to 3) |
  |---|---|
  | 5 s | 10 |
  | 15 s | 30 |
  | 30 s | 60 |
  | 2 min | 240 |
  | 10 min | 1,200 |

- **Inputs:** two rails. Crash point: 0 to 4 (five snap points, labelled in words). Downtime: 5 s, 15 s, 30 s, 2 min, 10 min (snap points, log spacing). Default: point 1 and 15 s.
- **What moves:** a short strip of the protocol steps with a lightning mark at the crash point. Two database boxes change state (Working, Prepared, Committed) and show a padlock while prepared. A queue box on the right fills with order dots as the downtime rail is dragged, with the count beside it. At point 3, a thin arrow from B to A shows "ask Stock".
- **Verdicts:**

  | When | Verdict |
  |---|---|
  | Point 0 | "Safe: both can abort alone" |
  | Point 1 | "Both stuck with locks held" |
  | Point 2 | "Both stuck until it reads its log" |
  | Point 3 | "Payments waits, or asks Stock" |
  | Point 4 | "Nothing stuck: both committed" |

- **Primitive:** `picture-slider.tsx` twice (crash point as a stepped rail, downtime as a log rail), with the database boxes drawn in the picture like the 10.x scene actors; `beat-frame.tsx` for the limits fold.
- **Sources:** S77 for the blocking claim. The 2 orders per second is a course example.

### sysdesign-m14-saga-fail (14.3 beat "Fail a step and watch the undo")
- **Claim:** When a saga step fails, the saga runs the undo steps of the finished steps, newest first. A step that cannot be undone goes last and is retried, not undone. Without undo steps the saga leaves a half-done order behind [S78].
- **Model:** Four steps, each with an undo:

  | Step | Action | Undo |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | Reserve stock | Release stock |
  | 2 | Charge card (18) | Refund card |
  | 3 | Book courier | Cancel booking |
  | 4 | Send receipt | None: retried until it works |

  The learner taps the step that fails (1 to 4) or "none". A toggle sets undo steps on or off.
- **Formulas (undo steps on):** the actions that ran are the steps before the failed one. The undo steps are the undo of each of those that has one, newest first. A failure at step 4 runs no undo steps and retries the receipt.
- **Check points:**

  | Fails at | Actions that ran | Undo steps, in order | Layla's card |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | none | 1, 2, 3, 4 | none | charged 18 |
  | 1 | none | none | not charged |
  | 2 | 1 | release stock | not charged |
  | 3 | 1, 2 | refund card, release stock | charged then refunded: net 0 |
  | 4 | 1, 2, 3 | none (retry the receipt) | charged 18 |

  With undo steps off: failing at 2 leaves stock held; failing at 3 leaves stock held and 18 charged (net 18, no order).
- **Inputs:** tap one of five chips: "Step 1", "Step 2", "Step 3", "Step 4", "None fail". A toggle: "Undo steps: on / off". Default: Step 3, on.
- **What moves:** four step cards in a row. Cards that ran turn solid green. The failed card turns red. Then undo cards slide in under the finished ones, right to left, with arrows showing the order. A small counter below shows "Card: net 0" or "Card: net 18" and "Stock: held" or "Stock: free".
- **Verdicts:**

  | When | Verdict |
  |---|---|
  | None fail | "All four steps committed" |
  | Step 1 fails | "Nothing to undo: step 1 failed" |
  | Step 2 fails | "1 undo step: release the stock" |
  | Step 3 fails | "2 undo steps, newest first" |
  | Step 4 fails, undo on | "No undo: retry the receipt" |
  | Step 3 fails, undo off | "Card charged, stock held, no order" |

- **Primitive:** `block-map.tsx` (tap cards, path highlight) for the step cards and the undo path; `beat-frame.tsx` for the limits fold. A state-machine renderer is also a fit if the builder prefers states.
- **Sources:** S78 for compensating transactions. The microservices.io saga page (to be added to the dossier) for the pattern.

### sysdesign-m14-outbox-crash (14.4 beat "Crash between the two writes")
- **Claim:** Writing to a database and to a broker in two steps can lose the event (save then publish) or send a ghost event (publish then save). An outbox row saved in the same transaction never loses it, but the relay can publish it twice [S79].
- **Model:** Three ways to save an order and announce it, and three crash points. Count the events that reach the broker for one order, and whether the order exists.
- **Check points:**

  | Approach | No crash | Crash between the two steps | Crash after publishing, before marking sent |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Save, then publish | 1 event, order saved | 0 events, order saved | not applicable |
  | Publish, then save | 1 event, order saved | 1 event, no order (ghost) | not applicable |
  | Outbox and relay | 1 event, order saved | 1 event, order saved (sent after the next relay run) | 2 events, order saved |

  For the outbox, "between the two steps" means after the commit and before the relay runs.
- **Inputs:** a three-way choice for the approach and a three-way choice for the crash point. The third crash point is disabled (greyed, with a hint) for the two approaches that have no relay. Default: "Save, then publish" and "Between the two steps".
- **What moves:** a sequence view with lanes for App, Database, Relay (only for the outbox) and Broker. The steps play one after another as arrows. A red cross marks the crash and stops the arrows after it. Under the broker lane, event dots pile up. A badge on the database lane shows "order saved" or "no order".
- **Verdicts:**

  | When | Verdict |
  |---|---|
  | No crash | "One event, one order" |
  | Save then publish, crash | "Order saved, event lost" |
  | Publish then save, crash | "Event for an order that is not there" |
  | Outbox, crash between | "Event sent later: nothing lost" |
  | Outbox, crash after publish | "Sent twice: the consumer must cope" |

- **Primitive:** `lane-sequence.tsx` for the lanes and arrows, with the choice chips of `beat-choice.tsx`; `beat-frame.tsx` for the limits fold.
- **Sources:** S79. The 500-order run in challenge 13 part 1 (64 events missing for the dual write, 0 missing and 103 repeats for the outbox) is the measured cousin.

### sysdesign-m14-dedupe-flow (14.5 beat "Count the copies, with and without a table")
- **Claim:** A consumer without protection applies every copy. A dedupe table written in a separate step still lets a copy through when the consumer crashes between the work and the record. An id saved in the same transaction as the work gives exactly one effect for any crash chance.
- **Model:** N = 100 messages. Let a be the chance that the ack is lost after the work, so the message is delivered again. Let c be the chance that the consumer crashes between the work and the record of the id.
- **Formulas (expected values):**
  - No dedupe: each delivery applies the effect, and a lost ack brings another delivery. Effects = N / (1 - a). Extra = N x a / (1 - a).
  - Dedupe table in a separate step: a copy after a recorded id is skipped. The effect repeats only when the crash fell in the gap: the id was not recorded, so the next delivery applies the effect again. Effects = N / (1 - c). Extra = N x c / (1 - c). The ack-loss chance a does not change this.
  - Dedupe in the same transaction: a crash inside the transaction undoes both the claim and the work, so effects = N. Extra = 0 for every a and c.
- **Check points (N = 100):**

  | a (ack lost) | c (gap crash) | No dedupe: effects | Separate table: effects | Same transaction: effects |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0% | 0% | 100 | 100 | 100 |
  | 5% | 0% | 105.3 | 100 | 100 |
  | 20% | 5% | 125 | 105.3 | 100 |
  | 20% | 10% | 125 | 111.1 | 100 |
  | 40% | 5% | 166.7 | 105.3 | 100 |

  Round to whole numbers on screen. The challenge 13 part 2 run (300 messages, a = 20%, c = 5%) gave 368, 324 and 300 reserved bags. The expected values for 300 are 375, 315.8 and 300, so a seeded run lands within about 10 of the formula.
- **Inputs:** a three-way choice for the consumer (no dedupe, table in a separate step, same transaction), and two rails: ack lost 0%, 5%, 10%, 20%, 40% (snap points), and gap crash 0%, 2%, 5%, 10% (snap points). The gap rail is greyed out for "no dedupe" and "same transaction". Default: no dedupe, 20%, 5%.
- **What moves:** a column of 100 message dots entering a consumer box. Copies appear as extra dots (a lighter colour) beside the originals. A counter under the consumer shows "effects applied" against "expected 100", and the difference is filled red. For the separate table, the dots that slip through the gap are outlined, with a small arrow into the gap between "work" and "record".
- **Verdicts:**

  | When | Verdict |
  |---|---|
  | No dedupe, a = 0 | "No copies, no harm" |
  | No dedupe, a above 0 | "Every copy counts: N extra" (N filled in) |
  | Separate table, c = 0 | "Fine until a crash hits the gap" |
  | Separate table, c above 0 | "The crash gap lets N through" (N filled in) |
  | Same transaction | "Exactly 100 effects at any chance" |

- **Primitive:** `traffic-playground.tsx` (arrivals and a consumer with a rate, a flow of dots) with the three-way choice of `beat-choice.tsx`, or `picture-slider.tsx` twice with the dot column drawn in the picture; `beat-frame.tsx` for the limits fold.
- **Sources:** S82, S83 for at-least-once delivery. The formulas are a geometric-series argument written out for this module; the challenge 13 run is the check.
