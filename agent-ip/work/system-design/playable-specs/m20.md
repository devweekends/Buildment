# Module 20 playable specs (URL shortener and paste service)

Four reserved slugs. The builder creates each folder (`frontend/src/canvas/<slug>/`), registers it and writes its `spec.md`. All four sit on `BeatFrame` (`primitives/beat-frame.tsx`), draw on a 420 wide viewBox with labels of at least 11.5 units, use `SvgNode` cards and `VerdictBanner`, take theme tokens only, and keep every verdict at 38 characters or fewer. At most two controls sit outside the picture, each a 44px target with a visible label; every other input is inside the picture. The ten watch-only scenes of the module (`sysdesign-m20-*`, kind `scene`) already exist as data in `frontend/src/canvas/`. They use the same names (Wasla, Redirect, Cache, Database) and the same numbers as the playables below, so the picture and the play agree.

Every number below was computed with code (the formulas are short and shown). All models are deterministic; none uses a random number at play time. Course numbers shared by all four, defined in chapter 20.1: 500 million new links a month; 100 redirects per link; peak 5 times the average; links kept 5 years; 300 bytes per link; a month is 2,592,000 seconds. Defined in chapters 20.2 and 20.4: an app server finishes at most 10,000 redirects a second and is planned at half (5,000); a database node finishes at most 5,000 operations a second and is planned at half (2,500); a cache hit costs 0.55 ms at the service and a miss 3.05 ms (0.05 proxy hop + 0.5 cache query + 2 database read + 0.5 store in cache).

## sysdesign-m20-estimate-play

- **Chapter and beat:** 20.1 beat 2, "Drag the volume, find the load".
- **Claim:** For a link shortener, creating links is small; opening them is the load, and storage over years is the second driver. The card with the biggest consequence is ringed.
- **Primitives:** `estimate-chain.tsx` (cards joined by links, some cards are inputs: tap, then drag sideways or use arrow keys), `VerdictBanner`. No toggle.
- **Input:** three draggable cards. New links per month: 100M, 200M, 500M, 1B, 2B (default 500M). Reads per link: 10, 50, 100, 200, 1,000 (default 100). Years kept: 1, 2, 5, 10 (default 5). The peak factor (5) and the bytes per link (300) are fixed cards labelled "assumption".
- **Model and formulas:**
  - Creates a second = new links / 2,592,000.
  - Redirects a second, average = creates x reads per link.
  - Redirects a second, peak = average x 5.
  - Links stored = new links x 12 x years.
  - Storage = links stored x 300 bytes.
- **Picture:** two chains that start at the same card. Upper chain: New links per month > (divide by 2,592,000 s) Creates a second > (x reads per link) Redirects a second, average > (x 5 peak) Redirects a second, peak. Lower chain: New links per month > (x 12 x years) Links stored > (x 300 B) Storage. Dots flow along the links at a speed set by the value. Format: `193 /s`, `19,300 /s`, `96,500 /s`, `30 billion`, `9 TB`. Ring (tone warn) on the driver card: the peak card when peak reads are 2,500 or more, otherwise the storage card when storage is 5 TB or more, otherwise none.
- **Numbers to check** (computed with the formulas above):

  | New links | Reads per link | Years | Creates /s | Peak redirects /s | Stored | Storage |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 500M | 100 | 5 | 192.9 | 96,451 | 30 billion | 9 TB |
  | 100M | 100 | 5 | 38.6 | 19,290 | 6 billion | 1.8 TB |
  | 2B | 100 | 5 | 771.6 | 385,802 | 120 billion | 36 TB |
  | 500M | 10 | 5 | 192.9 | 9,645 | 30 billion | 9 TB |
  | 500M | 1,000 | 5 | 192.9 | 964,506 | 30 billion | 9 TB |
  | 500M | 100 | 1 | 192.9 | 96,451 | 6 billion | 1.8 TB |
  | 500M | 100 | 10 | 192.9 | 96,451 | 60 billion | 18 TB |
  | 100M | 10 | 1 | 38.6 | 1,929 | 1.2 billion | 0.36 TB |

- **Verdicts:**
  - `Reads drive it: a cache is a must` (peak 50,000 or more; warn)
  - `Reads matter: a cache helps a lot` (peak 2,500 to 49,999; warn)
  - `Storage drives it: spread the data` (peak under 2,500 and storage 5 TB or more; warn)
  - `One database could serve this` (otherwise; good)
- **Reduced motion:** the dots do not flow; the cards show their final values.
- **Accessibility:** each input card is a `role="slider"` with value text such as "500 million new links a month, 193 creates a second, 96,451 redirects a second at peak, 9 terabytes"; the ring is also written as text in the verdict.
- **Sources:** Arithmetic on course assumptions (chapter 2.3 for the method). No external source.

## sysdesign-m20-design-stepper

- **Chapter and beat:** 20.2 beat 3, "Step through the design at peak".
- **Claim:** The numbers decide the blocks. App servers do nothing for the database; a cache cuts its load by the hit rate; partitions share what is left.
- **Primitives:** `design-stepper.tsx` with a `DesignSpec` (`models/design-stepper.ts`): five steps, Back and Next under the picture, step chips on top, one choice in step 4 (partition count, a row of three chips inside the picture). `SvgNode` cards, `VerdictBanner`.
- **Input:** Back and Next (or tap a step chip). In step 4 only: partition count 3, 5 or 8 (default 5).
- **Model:** peak load is 100,000 redirects and 1,000 creates a second (101,000 operations). Steps:
  1. One box: app and database together. Database operations 101,000 against a plan of 2,500.
  2. Add app servers: 100,000 / 5,000 = 20 servers. The database still gets 101,000.
  3. Add a cache with a 90% hit rate: the database gets 100,000 x 0.10 + 1,000 = 11,000.
  4. Split the data into k partitions by a hash of the code: each gets 11,000 / k.
  5. Count clicks off the path: a queue and a counter worker appear next to the redirect service. The database load is unchanged; the redirect path stays one trip on a hit.
- **Formulas:**
  - Times its plan = operations / 2,500.
  - Per partition share of plan = (11,000 / k) / 2,500.
  - App servers = ceil(100,000 / 5,000) = 20.
- **Picture:** left to right: Browsers > (App servers x N) > (Cache, from step 3) > (Database x k). A pill on the database card shows "40x its plan" (step 1 and 2), "4.4x its plan" (step 3) and the per-partition share in step 4. A pill on the app card shows the server count. Requests run through as dots; in step 3 nine in ten turn back at the cache. In step 5 a second dot leaves the app card toward a Queue card and a Counter card.
- **Numbers to check:**

  | Step | Database operations a second | Times its plan |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 101,000 | 40.4 |
  | 2 | 101,000 | 40.4 |
  | 3 | 11,000 | 4.4 |
  | 4, k = 3 | 3,667 each | 147% of plan |
  | 4, k = 5 | 2,200 each | 88% of plan |
  | 4, k = 8 | 1,375 each | 55% of plan |
  | 5 | 2,200 each (k = 5) | 88% of plan |

- **Verdicts:**
  - `Database at 40 times its plan` (step 1; bad)
  - `Apps cope, database still 40x over` (step 2; bad)
  - `Cache: database now 4.4 times plan` (step 3; warn)
  - `5 partitions: each at 88% of plan` (step 4, k = 5; good)
  - `3 partitions: each at 147% of plan` (step 4, k = 3; bad)
  - `8 partitions: each at 55% of plan` (step 4, k = 8; good; it says nothing about the cost of more nodes, the chapter fold does)
  - `Counting leaves the redirect path` (step 5; good)
- **Reduced motion:** dots are replaced by arrows with counts; the final frame of each step shows its pill.
- **Accessibility:** step chips and partition chips are buttons with visible labels; the database pill is read out as text.
- **Sources:** Course numbers (chapter 20.2 fold). The 2,500 plan follows chapter 2.5 (plan a node at about half busy).

## sysdesign-m20-collision-play

- **Chapter and beat:** 20.3 beat 2, "Drag the length, watch the clashes".
- **Claim:** For random codes, the share of codes already taken is the chance that a new code clashes. At 30 billion links 6 characters is more than half full and 7 is under 1%. The first clash comes after about the square root of the keyspace, long before it is full.
- **Primitives:** `picture-slider.tsx` for the number of stored links (log rail inside the picture), `snap-stops-rail.tsx` or `rail-slider.tsx` for the code length (inside the picture), `VerdictBanner`. No toggle.
- **Input:** code length L on a snap rail 4 to 9, default 6. Stored links n on a log rail with snap points 1 million, 100 million, 1 billion, 10 billion, 30 billion, default 30 billion. Arrow keys step 1 on L and one snap on n.
- **Model and formulas:**
  - Possible codes = 62^L. Share taken f = n / 62^L (full when f is 1 or more).
  - Extra tries per create = f / (1 - f). One create in 1 / f must retry.
  - Extra tries a second at 193 creates a second = 193 x f / (1 - f).
  - First clash expected after about 1.2533 x sqrt(62^L) links (marker on the n rail).
- **Picture:** a wide bar for the keyspace, labelled with its size ("3.5 trillion codes"); the taken share is shaded from the left (with a thin minimum width so a tiny share is still visible) and labelled with its percentage. Beneath it, a log rail for n with a marker "first clash expected here" at 1.2533 x sqrt(62^L). Two numbers next to the bar: "1 create in N retries" and "extra tries a second".
- **Numbers to check** (share taken, extra tries per create, first clash):

  | L | Possible codes | First clash about | 1M links | 100M | 1B | 30B |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 4 | 14.8 million | 4,818 | 6.8% | full | full | full |
  | 5 | 916 million | 37,934 | 0.11% | 11% | full | full |
  | 6 | 56.8 billion | 298,696 | 0.0018% | 0.18% | 1.8% | 53% (1.1 extra) |
  | 7 | 3.52 trillion | 2.4 million | 0.000028% | 0.0028% | 0.028% | 0.85% (0.0086 extra, 1 in 117) |
  | 8 | 218 trillion | 18.5 million | negligible | 0.000046% | 0.00046% | 0.014% (1 in 7,278) |
  | 9 | 13.5 quadrillion | 145.8 million | negligible | negligible | 0.0000074% | 0.00022% |

  At L = 7 and n = 30 billion: 193 x 0.0086 = 1.66 extra tries a second.
- **Verdicts:**
  - `More links than codes` (f is 1 or more; bad)
  - `1 create in 2 must retry` (f of 0.25 or more, with N = round(1 / f) filled in; bad)
  - `1 create in 57 must retry` (f from 0.01 to 0.25; warn)
  - `Clashes are rare; a retry covers it` (f under 0.01; good)
- **Reduced motion:** no animation; the bar and numbers update at once.
- **Accessibility:** each rail is a `role="slider"` with value text such as "7 characters, 30 billion links, 0.85 percent of codes taken, 1 create in 117 retries".
- **Sources:** Arithmetic. The square-root bound is the standard birthday bound (source needed, listed in the chapter). Possible codes 62^L are exact.

## sysdesign-m20-hit-rate-play

- **Chapter and beat:** 20.4 beat 2, "Drag the hit rate, watch the database".
- **Claim:** The average falls smoothly with the hit rate, but p99 stays at the miss time until the hit rate reaches 99%, and the database load and node count track the misses.
- **Primitives:** `picture-slider.tsx` (a rail inside the picture) for the hit rate, `log-ruler.tsx` or a plain linear ruler for the latency strip, `SvgNode` for the Cache and Database cards, `VerdictBanner`. No toggle.
- **Input:** hit rate h on a rail from 0 to 99, step 1, snap points 0, 50, 80, 90, 95, 99, default 90. Arrow keys step 1, Page keys step 10.
- **Model and formulas** (hit time 0.55 ms, miss time 3.05 ms, 100,000 redirects and 1,000 creates a second at peak, a database node planned for 2,500 operations):
  - Average = h x 0.55 + (1 - h) x 3.05 = 3.05 - 2.5 h (ms).
  - p99 = 0.55 ms if h is 99% or more, otherwise 3.05 ms.
  - Database operations = 100,000 x (1 - h) + 1,000 a second.
  - Nodes = ceil(operations / 2,500). Floating point can give 20,999.999999 for 21,000, so round the operations to the nearest whole number before dividing.
- **Picture:** top, a latency strip from 0 to 3.5 ms with two fixed ticks ("hit 0.55", "miss 3.05") and two markers: "average" (slides smoothly) and "p99" (jumps from the miss tick to the hit tick at 99). Bottom, a Cache card showing "hit 90%" and a Database card showing "11,000 /s" with a row of up to 41 small node icons, the needed ones lit. Requests flow as dots, the share that reaches the database set by 1 - h.
- **Numbers to check:**

  | Hit rate | Average | p99 | Database operations a second | Nodes |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0% | 3.05 ms | 3.05 ms | 101,000 | 41 |
  | 50% | 1.80 ms | 3.05 ms | 51,000 | 21 |
  | 80% | 1.05 ms | 3.05 ms | 21,000 | 9 |
  | 90% | 0.80 ms | 3.05 ms | 11,000 | 5 |
  | 95% | 0.68 ms | 3.05 ms | 6,000 | 3 |
  | 98% | 0.60 ms | 3.05 ms | 3,000 | 2 |
  | 99% | 0.58 ms | 0.55 ms | 2,000 | 1 |

- **Verdicts:**
  - `41 database nodes for misses` (h under 90, nodes filled in; bad when nodes are 10 or more, warn otherwise)
  - `Fast on average, p99 pays a miss` (h from 90 to 98; warn)
  - `p99 is now the cache time` (h is 99; good)
- **Reduced motion:** markers jump to their final place; no flowing dots.
- **Accessibility:** the rail is a `role="slider"` with value text "90 percent hits, average 0.80 ms, p99 3.05 ms, 11,000 database operations a second, 5 nodes".
- **Sources:** Latency building blocks from napkin-math [S3] (0.05 ms proxy hop, 0.5 ms cache query) and cache-aside's three trips on a miss [S26]; the 2 ms database read, the 2,500 node plan and the 100,000 and 1,000 peaks are course numbers.
