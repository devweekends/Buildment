## What works

- **Uncompromising adherence to verifiable proof:** The curriculum consistently enforces that every claim, metric, and bullet must originate from an audit-proof ledger row with clear before/after measurements and explicit attribution.
- **Deep empathy for low-bandwidth and mobile-first realities:** Technical recommendations systematically protect learners from predatory SaaS upsells, third-party platform lock-in, and reliance on fragile cloud demos.
- **Principled stance on platform etiquette and rules:** The module provides an exceptionally clear, rule-backed refutation of engagement pods, bots, and spam outreach, protecting fellows' professional reputations.
