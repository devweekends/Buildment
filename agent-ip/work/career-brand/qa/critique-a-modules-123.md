## What Works

- **Empathetic, highly realistic grounding for Gaza fellows:** The materials demonstrate exceptional contextual awareness for fellows facing intermittent electricity, expensive cellular bandwidth, and ESL barriers. Scenarios explicitly reject the "six-second" resume myth and AI-detection fads, replacing them with honest, defensible habits (e.g., accepting "I did not measure that" as a complete and respected answer).
- **Evidence-first architectural progression:** Rebuilding career narratives from raw facts and claim ledgers before writing a single line of prose effectively dismantles the cycle of empty buzzwords and unearned claims that cause remote applications to fail.
- **Provided fellow (Areej) dual-track scaffolding:** Maintaining a complete, parallel set of realistic artifacts for a fictional candidate ensures that learners without prior corporate employment, or who have confidentiality concerns regarding past employers, can fully participate in every exercise and reach every gate without friction.

---

## Recommendation

**Revise.** 

The pedagogical approach, narrative framing, and core instructional philosophy are outstanding and tailored precisely to the fellows' needs. However, publication is blocked by:
1. The technical failure in `01.06-checklist.md` (`minWords: 70` blocking audio submissions);
2. The truncated file in `02.04-the-human-part-and-asking-others.md`;
3. The pending G2B roadmap confirmation with 24 unresolved decisions;
4. Assessment contradictions in chapters 01.03 and 02.02.

Resolving these specific blockers and aligning the quiz keys will bring the course to reference-grade quality.
