# G2A critique, round 2, pedagogy and instructor lens: networking roadmap v2

## Verdict

Version 2 is a large improvement and most of round 1 is genuinely fixed in the roadmap itself, not only in the response note. Every one of the 27 Learn chapters now has a "Problem first" entry, the first network a learner builds arrives in 1.5 instead of 3.7, ping, ICMP and `tcpdump` are taught before they are used, the NAT teaser in 2.4 keeps the course question alive, the by-hand drills and the hint ladder exist, the capstone is four checkpointed parts, and I recomputed every module total, the slow-path column, the chapter counts and the 43, 36, 6 and 15 percent split: all are correct. No S1 remains. Two S2 defects remain, both new: chapter 1.4 got heavier (shell on-ramp, checker, install) without being re-timed or split, and the phone-only "Foundations" path depends on a `[replay]` piece that is not specified anywhere in the Interactive pieces section and says nothing about the five Code chapters that carry graded evidence for O2 to O5. There are also several smaller sequence and hook problems introduced by the reordering (a graded lab level and a capstone probe that ask for things taught later, early-course terms used before they are taught). Recommendation: revise once more with targeted fixes (the table below says exactly which), then send to instructors. A third full critique is not needed if the orchestrator confirms each fix against this list.

What works and should be kept: the module 3 and module 5 hooks (campus mask, "why can my phone not open localhost"), the 7.2 café and 7.4 outage hooks, the 6.3 hook, the "runs on" labels, the scaffold table (guided, gapped, open), the setup checker and `restore.sh`, the Code hint ladder with a reference-helper unlock, gate formats (written, voice or diagram), the final check before the demo, the published fault matrix, and the honest "tested / not yet tested" lines in Build environment.
