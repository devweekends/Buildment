# G2A critique, technical lens, round 2: networking roadmap v2

## Verdict

Roadmap v2 is a large improvement. Of the 21 round 1 defects, 13 are closed, 8 are partly closed and none are still open; every original S2 is now either closed or reduced to an S3 residue. The NAT story now matches the second execution test (dossier 5.6: address rewritten, port kept, port rewritten only on a clash, a masquerade-only router is not a firewall, inbound to a closed port is refused), the platform change for the network lab is described honestly, and the time totals add up (2,505 minutes, 75 chapters, counts of Learn, Code, Build and Lab chapters all match the tables). Two claims in the writer's response do not hold when checked against the roadmap: the "route experiment" from 6.3 and the `conntrack` pointer in 6.5 are not in the text. One new S2 remains: chapter 3.9 promises two specific ping errors and an unanswered ARP from a mask mismatch, which is not what two live Linux hosts normally produce and was never run. Nine new S3 defects are mostly about wording that outruns the evidence (port allocation, localhost, captive portals, unsourced numbers) and renderer limits (timeline unit, no prediction in `[sequence]`). None needs a structural change. Recommendation: revise narrowly (run the 3.9 scenario, fix N02 to N10), then send to instructors. A third full round is not needed.
