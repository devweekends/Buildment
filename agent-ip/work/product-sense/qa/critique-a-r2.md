# Critique A, round 2: Product Sense for Engineers (course 18)

## Method and limits

- I reviewed blind: the revised `course.md` (54 chapters), `canvases.md` (82 canvases, data and spec), the rubric, the playbook, the dossier and `revision-1-brief.md` as the list of what was supposed to change. I did not read the writers' rationale.
- I re-measured every figure the orchestrator gave me with my own scripts over `course.md`. All matched: 82 MCQs; the key is the strict longest in 20 of 82 (24%) and the strict shortest in 20 of 82 (24%); none is more than 10% longer than the longest wrong option; key positions 19, 22, 23, 18; "because" or "since" in 13 of 82 keys (16%) and 41 of 246 wrong options (17%); 25 predicts with the correct label longer in 13 (52%) and first in 14 (56%); 54 chapters; 1,552 minutes, and the module sums match the roadmap (212, 212, 237, 212, 187, 162, 330). My absolute-word list is wider than the orchestrator's (it adds all, every, must, nothing, no one): 8.5% of keys against 9.8% of wrong options, which is also neutral.
- New strategy tests on the 82 MCQs, all near chance (25%): the option most similar to the others picks the key 16 times (20%); the option that shares most words with the stem, 18 times (22%); the option with the most digits, 5 of 17 questions where one option has more. The cues I filed in round 1 are gone.
- I recomputed 80 stated contrast ratios in `course.md` and `canvases.md` by the WCAG formula (for example #8C8C8C 3.36, #666666 5.74, #7A7A7A on #F5F5F5 3.94, white on #4CAF50 2.78, #737373 on #EEEEEE 4.09, #8A8A8A 3.45, #767676 4.54). None is wrong. I also checked the bitrate arithmetic in 7.2 (22.5 MB, 37.5 MB), the 5-tester model in 4.5 (52% for two testers, so about 5 of 10), the 24 pixel circle reading in 3.4 Q1 (centres 28 apart) and the 2.2.2 reading in 3.5 (a 3 second spinner needs no pause control; an autoplay loop does).
- Not available to me: the course audit report, the canvas QA report and the rendered reader at 375px. I rely on the orchestrator for "54 of 54 validate, 0 errors, 0 warnings, 2,948 canvas tests pass". I did not open the server code that strips `modelAnswer`; I accept the orchestrator's cited path.
- Clean checks: zero en or em dashes in `course.md`, `canvases.md` and `course.yaml`; no AI attribution; sentences over 30 words are 23 of 6,734 and almost all are quoted follow-up questions; every Learn chapter has 4 to 5 beats; 107 of 110 beats are at 70 visible words or fewer (the three exceptions are in the new-defect list).
