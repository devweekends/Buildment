## 3. What Works (Preserve These Strengths)

1. **Zero typographic or lexical AI slop across 53 chapters and 36 canvases:** There is not a single em dash (`, `), en dash (` to `), `Co-Authored-By` trailer, or unquoted hype word (`delve`, `leverage`, `seamless`, `robust`, `transformative`) in the course's own voice. Bad AI copy (`"Revolutionize your printing experience"`, `"Seamless, powerful and effortless."`) appears strictly inside specimens that learners critique and rewrite.
2. **Exemplary epistemic honesty and dossier fidelity:** Every WCAG 2.2 criterion (`1.4.3`, `1.4.11`, `1.4.1`, `1.4.4`, `1.4.12`, `2.4.7`, `2.5.8`, `2.5.5`, `3.3.1`, `3.3.2`, `1.2.2`, `2.2.2`, `2.3.3`) is cited at its exact level (A, AA, or AAA) with AA kept as the course bar. Every contrast ratio matches the WCAG relative luminance formula to two decimal places (`#767676` = 4.54:1, `#777777` = 4.48:1, `#999999` = 2.85:1, `#595959` = 7.00:1, `#333333` = 12.63:1, `#A78BFA` = 2.72:1). Popular myths (three-click rule, Miller's 7±2 applied to menus, Hoober's retracted thumb-reach zones, F-pattern as a layout goal, 5 users finding all problems, dark mode being universally better for eyes) are carefully unpacked with their real study limits in `04.05`.
3. **Deep respect for Gaza learners' context:** Every module accounts for weak mobile data, phone-first reading, no paid AI accounts, and privacy/safety. Every `Build` and `Gate` chapter provides the complete, self-contained **Wasel** case (Sami's print shop near the university gate, Karam's one-evening AI app, 0.50 / 2 / 5 shekels, orders before noon ready by 4 pm) so a learner without a laptop, a live project, or an open AI tool can complete every exercise on paper or in a phone notes app.
4. **Actionable, non-judgmental pedagogy ("what you gave up"):** Requiring learners to record "what you gave up" in every critique log entry (`04.06`, `05.05`, `06.04`, `07.06`, `07.07`) turns subjective taste into concrete engineering trade-offs. Framing Karam's and Maryam's early AI-generated screens without mockery ("Karam did nothing wrong. The tool made the choices because he did not") keeps emotional safety high.
5. **High-value interactive canvases:** All 36 canvases are purpose-built manipulates (sliders for body size, line height, spacing groups, contrast ladder, wait-time ladder; toggles for squint/blur and hierarchy signals; timed 10-second skim tests in `product-m7-skim-before` and `product-m7-skim-after`; and card sorters with per-card wrong-bucket explanations).
